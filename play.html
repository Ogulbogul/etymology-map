How it works

  1. You see an English word and what it means today.
  2. Click the map to drop pins where you think the word travelled, oldest place first. Zoom in for accuracy.
  3. Points come from distance, not country. The closer each pin is to a real stop, the more you score, and pins in the right order earn a bonus.
  4. Five words per game, up to 5,000 points. No sign-up, and nothing is tied to you.
