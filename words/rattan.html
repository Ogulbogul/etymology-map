Read the full story

Rattan’s story begins in Malay: rotan meant “a climbing palm vine.” The flexible climbing palm used for weaving and furniture. It reached English by 1660s as rattan. Adopted directly for the material used in wickerwork. Today, rattan means a climbing palm whose stems are used for wickerwork and furniture.
