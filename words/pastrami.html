Pastrami

4 stages from Turkish to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a seasoned, smoked, and steamed cut of beef, typically sliced thin for sandwiches

Where does the word "pastrami" come from? It comes from Turkish basdirma / pastirma, passing through Romanian and Yiddish before entering English (1916).

basdirma / pastirma→pastrama→pastrame→pastrami
