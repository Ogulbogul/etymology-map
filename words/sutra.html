Read the full story

Sutra’s story begins in Sanskrit: sutra meant “a thread; an aphorism or rule.” Meant 'thread, string', from a root meaning 'to sew'. It reached English by 19th c. as sutra. Adopted in English writing on Hindu and Buddhist texts. Today, sutra means a concise aphorism or rule in Hindu, Buddhist, or Jain literature; more broadly, a sacred text or teaching.
