Read the full story

Cartel’s story begins in Italian: carta meant “a letter, a formal document.” A letter, a document, especially one formalizing an agreement between rivals. By 1600s, the word had taken hold in French as cartel. Carried into French for a written agreement between opposing parties. It reached English by 1900s as cartel. Narrowed to mean businesses or groups colluding to control a market. Today, cartel means an alliance of businesses or groups formed to limit competition and control a market.
