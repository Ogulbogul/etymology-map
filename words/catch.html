Read the full story

Catch’s story begins in Vulgar Latin: captare meant “to try to seize.” A frequentative of 'capere', to take. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old North French as cachier. The Norman form kept the hard 'k' sound. It reached English by 1200s as catch. Moved from chasing to actually capturing. Today, catch means to seize and hold something moving.
