Read the full story

Extravaganza’s story begins in Latin: extra + vagari meant “to wander outside, beyond.” 'outside' plus 'to wander' combined. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Italian as estravagante. Described something wandering beyond normal bounds. By 1700s, Italian speakers had reshaped it into extravaganza. A noun coined for extravagant, eccentric behavior. It reached English by 1754-1794 as extravaganza. Entered for peculiar behavior, then specialized to an elaborate, fantastical stage spectacle. Today, extravaganza means an elaborate, spectacular public entertainment or display.
