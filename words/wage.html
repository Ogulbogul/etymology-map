Read the full story

Wage’s story begins in Frankish: wadi meant “pledge.” A Frankish word related to English 'wed'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as wage. A security given for a promise. It reached English by 1300s as wage. 'wage war' keeps the old sense of pledging to fight. Today, wage means a fixed regular payment for work.
