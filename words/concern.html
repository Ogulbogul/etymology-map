Read the full story

Concern’s story begins in Medieval Latin: concernere meant “to relate to, to regard.” To sift together, to relate to, from com- (together) and cernere (to sift, distinguish). It reached English by 1400s as concern. Settled as the English word for relating to or worrying about something. Today, concern means a feeling of worry, or a matter that relates to or affects someone.
