Read the full story

Supreme’s story begins in Latin: supremus meant “highest.” Superlative of 'superus', above. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as suprême. Latin 'supremus' was the superlative of 'superus', situated above; 'Supreme Court' is recorded in English from 1689. It reached English by 1520s as supreme. The US Supreme Court dates from 1789. Today, supreme means highest in rank.
