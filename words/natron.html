Natron

5 stages from Egyptian to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means A naturally occurring mineral (sodium carbonate) historically used by ancient Egyptians in mummification, today used in chemistry and glassmaking.

Where does the word "natron" come from? It comes from Egyptian netjeryt, passing through Ancient Greek, Arabic and French before entering English (1680s).
