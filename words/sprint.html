Read the full story

Sprint’s story begins in Old Norse: spretta meant “to spring, to leap.” Related to Swedish 'sprinta', to jump; athletics sprinting dates from the 1800s. It reached English by 1560s as sprint. Settled as the English word for running at full speed over a short distance. Today, sprint means to run at full speed over a short distance.
