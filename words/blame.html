Read the full story

Blame’s story begins in Late Latin: blasphemare meant “to revile.” From Greek 'blasphēmein', to speak evil, the root of 'blasphemy'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as blasmer. Shortened form of the church word. It reached English by 1200s as blame. 'blasphemy' was later borrowed separately, so English has both. Today, blame means to hold responsible for a fault.
