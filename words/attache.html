Read the full story

Attache’s story begins in French: attacher meant “to attach.” To fasten, attach. Within French, it grew into attaché by 1800s. 'attached': a junior diplomat attached to an embassy. It reached English by 1830s as attaché. The 'attaché case', a slim document case, dates from 1904. Today, attache means a person on the staff of an ambassador.
