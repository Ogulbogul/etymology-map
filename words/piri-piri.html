Read the full story

Piri piri’s story begins in Swahili/Bantu: pili pili meant “a chili pepper.” The word for a small, fiery chili pepper. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in Portuguese as piri piri. Adopted by Portuguese traders along the East African coast. It reached English by 1900s as piri piri. Adopted directly for the spicy pepper and its namesake sauce. Today, piri piri means a hot chili pepper sauce, popular in Portuguese and African-influenced cuisine.
