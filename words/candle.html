Read the full story

Candle’s story begins in Latin: candela meant “candle.” From 'candere', to shine. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old English as candel. One of the first church words borrowed by the Anglo-Saxons. It reached English by 1200s as candle. 'can't hold a candle to' dates from the 1600s. Today, candle means a wax stick with a wick burned for light.
