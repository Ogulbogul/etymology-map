Read the full story

Brick’s story begins in Middle Dutch: brikke meant “slab.” A Middle Dutch word, related to 'break': a broken piece. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as brique. Brick-making spread from Flanders. It reached English by 1400s as brick. 'brick' for a reliable person is 1840s slang. Today, brick means a block of baked clay for building.
