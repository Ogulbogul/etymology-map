Read the full story

Mart’s story begins in Middle Dutch: marct meant “market.” A market, from Latin 'mercātus'. It reached English by 1400s as mart. Borrowed through the wool and cloth trade with Flanders; revived in shop names like Kmart in the 1960s. Today, mart means a trading centre or market.
