Read the full story

Pagan’s story begins in Latin: paganus meant “villager, rustic.” From 'pagus', country district. By ancient, the word had taken hold in Late Latin as paganus. Christians used it for those outside the faith; English took it directly from Latin. It reached English by 1300s as pagan. The neo-pagan revival dates from the 1900s. Today, pagan means a follower of a non-mainstream religion.
