Read the full story

Cricket’s story begins in Old French: criquet meant “a stick.” A stick, possibly related to a game played with a stick and ball. It reached English by 1590s as cricket. Settled as the English name for the bat-and-ball sport. Today, cricket means a bat-and-ball team sport, especially popular in Britain and its former colonies.
