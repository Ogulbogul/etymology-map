Durbar

3 stages from Persian to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a formal reception or ceremonial gathering held by an Indian prince or, historically, a British colonial official

Where does the word "durbar" come from? It comes from Persian dar + bar, passing through Hindi/Urdu before entering English (1609).

dar + bar→darbar→durbar
