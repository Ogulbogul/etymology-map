Read the full story

Muskellunge’s story begins in Ojibwe: maashkinoozhe meant “ugly pike.” Literally 'ugly pike', naming the large predatory freshwater fish. By 1700s, the word had taken hold in Canadian French as masquinongé. Adapted by French-Canadian trappers and fishers in the Great Lakes region. It reached English by 1780s-1790s as muskellunge. Anglicized from the French-Canadian form. Today, muskellunge means a large predatory freshwater fish of the pike family, prized by anglers in the Great Lakes region.
