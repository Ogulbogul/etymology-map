Read the full story

Burqa’s story begins in Arabic: burqu' meant “full veil.” Named a veil covering the face and body. By 1700s, the word had taken hold in Persian/Urdu as burqa. Adopted into South and Central Asian dress traditions. It reached English by 1800s as burqa. Entered English through accounts of Islamic dress across the Middle East and South Asia. Today, burqa means a loose garment covering the entire body and face, worn by some Muslim women.
