Read the full story

Momentum’s story begins in Latin: movimentum meant “movement.” Movement, from movere (to move). It reached English by 1690s as momentum. Adopted directly as the physics term for mass in motion. Today, momentum means the quantity of motion of a moving object, or figuratively, forward drive.
