Read the full story

Innovate’s story begins in Latin: novus meant “new.” New, combined with in- (into). Within Latin, it grew into innovare by ancient. To renew, to introduce new things, from in- and novus. It reached English by 1500s as innovate. Settled as the English word for introducing new methods or ideas. Today, innovate means to introduce new methods, ideas, or products.
