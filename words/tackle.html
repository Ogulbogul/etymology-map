Read the full story

Tackle’s story begins in Middle Dutch: takel meant “rigging.” A ship's rigging and hoisting gear. By 1200s, the word had taken hold in Middle English as takel. Gear or equipment, especially a ship's. It reached English by 1500s as tackle. The sporting sense, to seize an opponent, dates from the 1870s. Today, tackle means equipment for a task; to deal with a problem.
