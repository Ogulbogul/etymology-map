Read the full story

Charpoy’s story begins in Persian: chahār-pāy meant “four-footed.” 'four feet'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Urdu as chārpāī. The everyday string bed of South Asia. It reached English by 1840s as charpoy. Borrowed in British India. Today, charpoy means a light bedstead of webbing on a wooden frame.
