Read the full story

Jacuzzi’s story begins in Italian: Jacuzzi meant “a family name.” A family from Casarsa della Delizia in Friuli; seven brothers emigrated to California in the 1900s-1920s and built aircraft propellers and pumps. It reached English by 1968 as Jacuzzi. Candido Jacuzzi adapted a pump in 1956 to give hydrotherapy to his son, who had rheumatoid arthritis; Roy Jacuzzi launched the integrated whirlpool bath in 1968. Today, jacuzzi means a large bath with underwater jets of water (trademark).
