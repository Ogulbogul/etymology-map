Read the full story

Dolmus’s story begins in Turkish: dolmuş meant “filled.” 'filled', because it sets off only when every seat is taken. It reached English by 1960s as dolmus. Introduced to English readers by travel guides to Turkey. Today, dolmus means a shared minibus or taxi in Turkey that leaves when it is full.
