Schizophrenia

3 stages from Greek to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a serious mental disorder affecting how a person thinks, feels, and perceives reality

Where does the word "schizophrenia" come from? It comes from Greek schizein, passing through German before entering English (1912).
