Read the full story

Poke’s story begins in Hawaiian: poke meant “to slice crosswise.” Literally to slice or cut crosswise, describing cubed raw fish. It reached English by 1970s as poke. Hawaiian 'to slice'; poke bowls became a mainland US trend in the 2010s. Today, poke means a Hawaiian dish of marinated, cubed raw fish, typically served over rice.
