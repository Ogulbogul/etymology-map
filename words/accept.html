Read the full story

Accept’s story begins in Latin: acceptare meant “to take willingly.” To take willingly, from ad- (to) and capere (to take). It reached English by 1300s as accept. First recorded before 1382 in the Wycliffite Bible, translated from the Latin Vulgate. Today, accept means to willingly receive or agree to something offered.
