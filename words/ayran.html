Read the full story

Ayran’s story begins in Turkic: ayran meant “yogurt drink.” Made by Turkic nomads from soured milk, long before it reached Anatolia. By 1900s, the word had taken hold in Turkish as ayran. Turkey's prime minister declared it the national drink in 2013. It reached English by 1900s as ayran. Entered English through Turkish restaurants and kebab shops. Today, ayran means a cold salted drink of yogurt mixed with water.
