Read the full story

Baptize’s story begins in Late Latin: baptizare meant “to baptize.” From Greek 'baptizein', to dip or immerse. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as baptiser. John the Baptist immersed people in the Jordan. It reached English by 1200s as baptize. 'baptism of fire' dates from the 1800s. Today, baptize means to perform the Christian rite of baptism.
