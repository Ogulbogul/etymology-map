Read the full story

Saluki’s story begins in Arabic: saluqi meant “of Saluq, a swift hunting dog.” Named for Saluq, an ancient city in Arabia associated with the breed. It reached English by 1809 as saluki. From Saluq, a town in Yemen; one of the oldest dog breeds, painted in Egyptian tombs. Today, saluki means a breed of tall, swift hunting dog with a silky coat, one of the oldest known dog breeds.
