Read the full story

Apparel’s story begins in Latin: appariculare meant “to make fit.” From 'par', equal: to make things match. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as apareil. Furnishings and gear. It reached English by 1200s as apparel. Narrowed to clothing by the 1300s. Today, apparel means clothing.
