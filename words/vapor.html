Read the full story

Vapor’s story begins in Latin: vapor meant “steam.” Possibly related to Greek 'kapnos', smoke. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as vapour. Exhalation or mist. It reached English by 1300s as vapor. 'vaporware' dates from the 1980s. Today, vapor means a substance in gas form.
