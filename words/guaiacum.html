Guaiacum

3 stages from Taino to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a very hard, resinous tropical tree, also known as lignum vitae, historically valued for its medicinal resin

Where does the word "guaiacum" come from? It comes from Taino guayacán, passing through Spanish before entering English (1530s).

guayacán→guayaco→guaiacum
