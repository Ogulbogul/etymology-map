Hartal

4 stages from Sanskrit to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means A general strike or mass shutdown of shops and businesses, used as a form of political protest in South Asia.

Where does the word "hartal" come from? It comes from Sanskrit hatta, passing through Sanskrit and Gujarati before entering English (early 1900s).
