Read the full story

Mystery’s story begins in Greek: mysterion meant “a secret religious rite.” A secret rite known only to initiates of a religious cult. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as mystere. Carried into French for a hidden religious truth. It reached English by 1300s as mystery. Broadened from religious secrets to anything unexplained. Today, mystery means something that is difficult or impossible to explain or understand.
