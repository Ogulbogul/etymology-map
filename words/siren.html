Read the full story

Siren’s story begins in Greek: seiren meant “a mythical enchanting sea creature.” A mythical sea creature whose enchanting song lured sailors to shipwreck. It reached English by 1300s as siren. Broadened over time to mean any alluring danger, and later a loud warning device. Today, siren means a loud warning device, or a mythical sea creature that lures sailors with song.
