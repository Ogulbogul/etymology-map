Read the full story

Telescope’s story begins in Greek: tele meant “far, distant.” Far, distant, combined with skopein (to view). By 1610s, the word had taken hold in Italian as telescopio. Coined in Italy shortly after Galileo's refinements to the new viewing instrument. It reached English by 1640s as telescope. Settled as the English word for the instrument for viewing distant objects. Today, telescope means an optical instrument used to view distant objects, especially in astronomy.
