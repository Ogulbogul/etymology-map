Loquat

2 stages from Cantonese to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a small, sweet-tart orange fruit borne by an evergreen tree originally native to China

Where does the word "loquat" come from? It comes from Cantonese lòuh gwāt, adopted directly into English (1800s).
