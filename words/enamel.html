Read the full story

Enamel’s story begins in Frankish: smalt meant “melted glass.” A Frankish word related to 'smelt'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as esmail. Limoges was famous for enamel work. It reached English by 1400s as enamel. Tooth enamel dates from the 1700s. Today, enamel means a glassy coating; the hard layer on teeth.
