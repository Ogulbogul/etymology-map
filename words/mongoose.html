Read the full story

Mongoose’s story begins in Marathi/Telugu: mangus meant “mongoose.” The name for the small, snake-hunting mammal. It reached English by 1600s as mongoose. Kipling's 'Rikki-Tikki-Tavi' (1894) made the snake-fighting mongoose famous. Today, mongoose means a small, agile mammal known for its ability to kill venomous snakes.
