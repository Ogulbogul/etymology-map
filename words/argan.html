Argan

3 stages from Berber to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means Argan oil, pressed from the kernels of the Moroccan argan tree, used in cooking and cosmetics.

Where does the word "argan" come from? It comes from Berber argan, passing through French before entering English (2000s).
