Read the full story

Motto’s story begins in Late Latin: muttum meant “a word, utterance.” Meaning "a grunt" or "a word". By medieval, the word had taken hold in Italian as motto. Came to mean a short guiding phrase. It reached English by 1500s-1600s as motto. Adopted directly from Italian. Today, motto means a short phrase expressing a guiding principle or belief.
