Read the full story

Porch’s story begins in Latin: porticus meant “colonnade.” From 'porta', gate; the root of 'portico'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as porche. A covered area before a church door. It reached English by 1200s as porch. American 'porch' means a veranda. Today, porch means a covered shelter at the entrance to a building.
