Catamaran’s story begins in Tamil: kattumaram meant “tied logs.” Literally "tied wood", describing logs lashed together. It reached English by 1600s-1700s as catamaran. First recorded in 1697 in the buccaneer-explorer William Dampier's 'New Voyage Round the World'; Tamil 'tied wood', a raft of lashed logs used by Coromandel Coast fishermen. Today, catamaran means a boat with two parallel hulls.
