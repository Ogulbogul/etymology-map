Read the full story

Spread’s story begins in Old English: spredan meant “to extend, to stretch out.” To extend, to stretch out. It reached English by 1200s as spread. Settled as the modern English word for extending over an area. Today, spread means to extend over a wider area, or a paste for spreading on food.
