Jungle

3 stages from Sanskrit to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a dense tropical forest, or a wild, chaotic place

Where does the word "jungle" come from? It comes from Sanskrit jāṅgala, passing through Hindi before entering English (c. 1770s).

jāṅgala→jangal→jungle
