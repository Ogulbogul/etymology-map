Bulletin

5 stages from Latin to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a short official statement or announcement, especially of news

Where does the word "bulletin" come from? It comes from Latin bulla, passing through Italian and French before entering English (1765).

bulla→bulletta→bulletino→bulletin→bulletin
