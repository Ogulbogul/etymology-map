Admiral

4 stages from Arabic to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a high-ranking commissioned officer in a navy

Where does the word "admiral" come from? It comes from Arabic amīr al-baḥr, passing through Medieval Latin and Old French before entering English (c. 1400s).
