Read the full story

Pemmican’s story begins in Cree: pimikan meant “a preserved meat and fat mixture.” From "pimii" (fat, grease), a concentrated mix of dried meat and fat. It reached English by 1700s-1800s as pemmican. Cree 'pimîhkân', from 'pimiy', fat; the Hudson's Bay and North West companies fed their voyageurs on it. Today, pemmican means a concentrated mixture of dried meat, fat, and sometimes berries, used as a durable food.
