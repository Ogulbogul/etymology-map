Read the full story

Umbrella’s story begins in Latin: umbra meant “shade, shadow.” Meaning "shade" or "shadow". By medieval, the word had taken hold in Italian as ombrella. A diminutive meaning "little shade", a portable sunshade. It reached English by 1600s as umbrella. Jonas Hanway, who carried one in London from the 1750s, made the umbrella respectable for men. Today, umbrella means a folding device with a canopy used for protection from rain or sun.
