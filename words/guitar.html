Read the full story

Guitar’s story begins in Greek: kithara meant “a stringed instrument.” An ancient stringed instrument related to the lyre. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Arabic as qitara. Carried through Arabic-influenced Spain into European instrument vocabulary. From there it passed into Spanish as guitarra by medieval. Settled in Spanish for the fretted, stringed instrument. It reached English by 1600s as guitar. Adopted directly for the fretted, six-stringed instrument. Today, guitar means a stringed musical instrument played by plucking or strumming.
