Read the full story

Dungeon’s story begins in Late Latin: dominionem meant “lordship.” From 'dominus', lord, the same root as 'dominion' and 'domain'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as donjon. The lord's great tower, the strongest and most central part of a castle. It reached English by 1300s as dungeon. The prison cells under the keep eventually took over the whole meaning. Today, dungeon means a dark underground prison cell, especially in a castle.
