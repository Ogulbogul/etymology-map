Godown

4 stages from Tamil to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means A warehouse or storeroom, a term still used in parts of South and East Asia.

Where does the word "godown" come from? It comes from Tamil kidangu, passing through Malay and Portuguese before entering English (late 1500s).
