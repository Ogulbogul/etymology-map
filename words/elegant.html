Read the full story

Elegant’s story begins in Latin: elegans meant “choosy, refined.” From 'eligere', to pick out, the root of 'elect'. By 1400s, the word had taken hold in French as elegant. A learned borrowing used of fine style. It reached English by 1480s as elegant. A mathematical proof can also be 'elegant'. Today, elegant means graceful and stylish.
