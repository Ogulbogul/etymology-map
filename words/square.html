Read the full story

Square’s story begins in Vulgar Latin: exquadrare meant “to make square.” 'ex-' plus 'quadra', square, from 'quattuor', four. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as esquarre. A carpenter's set square. It reached English by 1200s as square. 'square' for dull and conventional is 1940s jazz slang. Today, square means a shape with four equal sides and right angles.
