Mate

3 stages from Quechua to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a caffeinated South American herbal tea, traditionally sipped through a metal straw from a hollow gourd

Where does the word "mate" come from? It comes from Quechua mati, passing through Spanish (Rioplatense) before entering English (1800s).
