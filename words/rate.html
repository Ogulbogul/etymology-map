Read the full story

Rate’s story begins in Medieval Latin: rata meant “fixed.” From 'pro rata parte', according to a fixed part. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as rate. A fixed amount. It reached English by 1400s as rate. 'first-rate' comes from the ranking of warships. Today, rate means a measure of speed or cost.
