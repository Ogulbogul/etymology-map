Read the full story

Armada’s story begins in Latin: armata meant “armed force.” By medieval, the word had taken hold in Spanish as armada. Came to mean a fleet of warships. It reached English by 1580s as armada. Adopted into English following the Spanish Armada of 1588. Today, armada means a large fleet of warships, or any large group of vehicles.
