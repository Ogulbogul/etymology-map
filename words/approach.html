Read the full story

Approach’s story begins in Late Latin: appropiare meant “to come near.” 'ad-' plus 'prope', near, the root of 'propinquity'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as aprochier. Used of troops advancing on a town. It reached English by 1300s as approach. 'approach' as a method dates from the 1900s. Today, approach means to come nearer; a way of dealing with something.
