Read the full story

Hound’s story begins in Proto-Germanic: hundaz meant “dog.” Cognate with Greek 'kyōn' and Latin 'canis'. By 700s-1000s, the word had taken hold in Old English as hund. The everyday word for any dog, until 'dog' took over. It reached English by 1500s as hound. Narrowed to hunting dogs; the verb 'to hound' dates from the 1520s. Today, hound means a dog, especially one used for hunting.
