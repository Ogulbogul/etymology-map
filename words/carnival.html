Read the full story

Carnival’s story begins in Latin: carne levare meant “to remove meat.” Literally "to remove meat", marking the start of Lent's fasting. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Italian as carnevale. Named the festive period before Lent's fasting began. It reached English by 1500s-1600s as carnival. Adopted directly from Italian. Today, carnival means a public festival or celebration, often with music, costumes, and parades.
