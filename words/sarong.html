Read the full story

Sarong’s story begins in Malay: sarung meant “a sheath, covering.” Literally "a sheath" or "covering". It reached English by 1800s as sarong. Malay 'sarung', sheath; Dorothy Lamour's 1930s films made it popular in the West. Today, sarong means a garment consisting of a length of fabric wrapped around the waist.
