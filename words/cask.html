Read the full story

Cask’s story begins in Late Latin: quassicare meant “to shake, break.” A probable source, through Spanish 'casco', a potsherd or helmet. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as casque. 'casque' meant a helmet; the barrel sense crossed over in English. It reached English by 1500s as cask. Wine casks shaped taste in winemaking. Today, cask means a large barrel.
