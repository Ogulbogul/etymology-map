Read the full story

Perestroika’s story begins in Russian: perestroika meant “restructuring.” Pere- 're-' + stroika 'building'; Mikhail Gorbachev made it the watchword of reform at the 27th Party Congress in 1986. It reached English by 1986 as perestroika. Gorbachev's 1987 book Perestroika: New Thinking for Our Country and the World became a Western bestseller. Today, perestroika means the economic and political restructuring of the Soviet Union in the late 1980s.
