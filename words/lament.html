Read the full story

Lament’s story begins in Latin: lamentum meant “wailing.” From an imitative root for crying out. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as lamenter. To wail in grief. It reached English by 1500s as lament. The Book of Lamentations mourns Jerusalem. Today, lament means to express grief.
