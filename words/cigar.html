Read the full story

Cigar’s story begins in Spanish: cigarro meant “a rolled tobacco leaf for smoking.” Possibly from a Mayan word for smoking tobacco leaves. It reached English by 1730s as cigar. Settled as the English word for the rolled tobacco product. Today, cigar means a roll of cured tobacco leaves for smoking.
