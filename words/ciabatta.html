Read the full story

Ciabatta’s story begins in Italian: ciabatta meant “slipper.” Slipper, describing the bread's long, flat, slipper-like shape. It reached English by 1990s as ciabatta. Adopted directly for the flat, airy Italian bread. Today, ciabatta means a flat, airy Italian bread with a crisp crust, shaped like a slipper.
