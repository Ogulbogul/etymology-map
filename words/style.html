Read the full story

Style’s story begins in Latin: stilus meant “writing stake.” A pointed tool for writing on wax tablets; the y came from confusion with Greek 'stylos', pillar. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as stile. Came to mean a manner of expression. It reached English by 1300s as style. The fashion sense dates from the 1800s. Today, style means a distinctive manner; elegance.
