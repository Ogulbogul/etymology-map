Read the full story

Tsetse’s story begins in Tswana: tsetse meant “tsetse fly.” An imitative name for the biting insect. It reached English by 1800s as tsetse. Adopted through European contact with southern Africa. Today, tsetse means a blood-feeding African fly that can transmit sleeping sickness.
