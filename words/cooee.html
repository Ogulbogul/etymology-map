Read the full story

Cooee’s story begins in Dharug: guu-wi meant “come here, a signal call.” A loud, far-carrying call used to attract attention across distance. It reached English by 1790s as cooee. Dharug for 'come here'; first recorded in a 1790 vocabulary of the Sydney language compiled by early colonists, then used as a bush call across Australia. Today, cooee means a loud, far-carrying call used to attract attention, especially in the Australian bush.
