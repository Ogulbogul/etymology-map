Read the full story

Bushel’s story begins in Gaulish: bostia meant “handful.” A Gaulish word. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as boissel. A measure for grain. It reached English by 1300s as bushel. 'hide your light under a bushel' is from the Gospels. Today, bushel means a measure of capacity for grain and fruit.
