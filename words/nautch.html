Nautch

3 stages from Sanskrit to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means A traditional style of dance performance in South Asia, historically performed by professional dancing girls for entertainment.

Where does the word "nautch" come from? It comes from Sanskrit nritya, passing through Hindi before entering English (1809).
