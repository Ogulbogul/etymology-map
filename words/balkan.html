Read the full story

Balkan’s story begins in Ottoman Turkish: balkan meant “wooded mountains.” The Turkish name for the Stara Planina range that crosses Bulgaria. By 1808, the word had taken hold in German as Balkanhalbinsel. The geographer August Zeune extended the mountain name to the whole 'Balkan Peninsula' in 1808. It reached English by 1800s as Balkan. The mountain-range name had reached English by 1794 for the Stara Planina alone; the peninsula-wide sense followed Zeune's German coinage. Today, balkan means relating to the Balkan Peninsula in southeastern Europe, or the region itself.
