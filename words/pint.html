Read the full story

Pint’s story begins in Vulgar Latin: pinta meant “painted mark.” Possibly from 'pingere', to paint: a mark on a vessel showing a measure. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as pinte. A measure of volume. It reached English by 1300s as pint. 'a pint' became shorthand for a glass of beer. Today, pint means a unit of liquid equal to one-eighth of a gallon.
