Read the full story

Panchayat’s story begins in Sanskrit: pancha meant “five.” Meant 'five'. Within Sanskrit, it grew into ayatana by ancient. Meant 'seat, abode, resting place'. From there it passed into Hindi as panchayat by medieval. Named a village council, originally of five members. It reached English by 1880s as panchayat. Adopted by British administrators to describe local Indian self-government. Today, panchayat means a local self-governing council at the village or small-town level in India and neighboring countries.
