Read the full story

Audacious’s story begins in Latin: audax meant “bold.” From 'audere', to dare. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as audacieux. It reached English by 1550s as audacious. 'audacity' followed. Today, audacious means showing a willingness to take bold risks.
