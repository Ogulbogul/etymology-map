Peyote

3 stages from Nahuatl to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a small, spineless cactus containing psychoactive compounds, used ceremonially by some Indigenous peoples

Where does the word "peyote" come from? It comes from Nahuatl peyotl, passing through Spanish before entering English (1800s).
