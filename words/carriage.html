Read the full story

Carriage’s story begins in Late Latin: carricare meant “to load.” From 'carrus', wagon. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as cariage. Norman French form. It reached English by 1300s as carriage. Horse-drawn 'carriages' date from the 1500s. Today, carriage means a vehicle; the way one carries oneself.
