Read the full story

Vermouth’s story begins in German: Wermut meant “wormwood.” Wormwood, the bitter herb historically used to flavor fortified wine. By 1800s, the word had taken hold in French as vermout. Adapted into French for the aromatic fortified wine. It reached English by 1806 as vermouth. Adopted directly for the herb-flavored fortified wine. Today, vermouth means a fortified wine flavored with aromatic herbs and spices.
