Read the full story

Plaza’s story begins in Latin: platea meant “broad street, open area.” Meaning "broad street". By medieval, the word had taken hold in Spanish as plaza. Came to mean a public town square. It reached English by 1800s as plaza. Spanish colonial towns were laid out around a central plaza under the Laws of the Indies (1573). Today, plaza means a public square or open area in a town or city.
