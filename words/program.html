Read the full story

Program’s story begins in Greek: programma meant “public notice.” 'pro', before, plus 'graphein', to write: a public notice posted in advance. By 1700s, the word had taken hold in French as programme. A list of events or performers. It reached English by 1940s as program. Applied to computer instructions; British English keeps 'program' only for this sense. Today, program means a planned series of events; a set of instructions for a computer.
