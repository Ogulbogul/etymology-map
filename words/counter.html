Read the full story

Counter’s story begins in Latin: computare meant “to reckon.” The root of 'count' and 'compute'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as conteor. A table where money was counted. It reached English by 1300s as counter. Shop 'counters' date from the 1600s. Today, counter means a flat surface for serving customers.
