Read the full story

Banzai’s story begins in Japanese: banzai meant “a shout of celebration or long life.” Literally 'ten thousand years', a traditional cheer wishing long life to the emperor. It reached English by 1940s as banzai. Entered wartime English describing Japanese soldiers' charging cry. Today, banzai means a Japanese cheer of celebration, or (in English) a reckless headlong charge.
