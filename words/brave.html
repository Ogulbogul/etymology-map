Read the full story

Brave’s story begins in Latin: barbarus meant “foreign, wild.” From Greek 'barbaros', one who babbles in a foreign tongue. By 1400s, the word had taken hold in Italian as bravo. Meant bold or wild; came into English through French 'brave'. It reached English by 1480s as brave. 'Bravo!' as praise shares the root. Today, brave means ready to face danger.
