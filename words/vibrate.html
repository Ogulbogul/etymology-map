Read the full story

Vibrate’s story begins in Latin: vibrare meant “to shake.” Possibly related to 'vibex', a weal. It reached English by 1610s as vibrate. 'vibes' as feelings dates from the 1960s. Today, vibrate means to move back and forth rapidly.
