Read the full story

Sequoia’s story begins in Cherokee: Sequoyah meant “a Cherokee leader's name.” Named after the Cherokee scholar who created the Cherokee syllabary. It reached English by 1800s as sequoia. Botanists named the giant redwood tree genus in his honor. Today, sequoia means a very large evergreen redwood tree native to California.
