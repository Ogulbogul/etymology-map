Read the full story

Woodchuck’s story begins in Cree (Algonquian): otchek meant “a burrowing rodent.” The name for the burrowing rodent, reshaped by folk etymology into "woodchuck". It reached English by 1600s-1700s as woodchuck. Adopted and reshaped by English colonists in North America. Today, woodchuck means a burrowing rodent native to North America, also known as a groundhog.
