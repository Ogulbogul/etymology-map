Read the full story

Kanga’s story begins in Swahili: kanga meant “speckled guineafowl.” The printed cotton cloth was named for its resemblance to the speckled bird's markings. It reached English by 1900s as kanga. Each kanga carries a printed Swahili proverb or saying. Today, kanga means a brightly coloured, patterned cotton cloth worn as a wrap in East Africa.
