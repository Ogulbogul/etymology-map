Read the full story

Ratatouille’s story begins in Occitan/French: touiller meant “to stir, to toss together.” 'rata' was French army slang for stew. By 1770s, the word had taken hold in French as ratatouille. Settled in French for the stewed mixed vegetable dish of Provence. It reached English by 1900s as ratatouille. Pixar's 2007 film 'Ratatouille' made the dish world-famous. Today, ratatouille means a French stewed dish of vegetables, including eggplant, zucchini, and tomato.
