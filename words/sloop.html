Read the full story

Sloop’s story begins in Dutch: sloep meant “small boat.” Possibly from 'sluipen', to glide or sneak. It reached English by 1620s as sloop. Later a small single-masted sailing boat or small warship. Today, sloop means a one-masted sailing boat.
