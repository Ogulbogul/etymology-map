Read the full story

Heir’s story begins in Latin: heres meant “heir.” The root of 'heredity' and 'inherit'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as eir. The h is silent, as in French. It reached English by 1300s as heir. 'heir apparent' dates from the 1300s. Today, heir means a person legally entitled to inherit.
