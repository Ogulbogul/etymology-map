Read the full story

Courtesy’s story begins in Latin: cohors meant “enclosed yard.” A farmyard or enclosure, which gave 'court', a royal household. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as cortoisie. The refined behaviour expected at a lord's court. It reached English by 1200s as courtesy. 'curtsy' is a shortened form of the same word. Today, courtesy means polite, considerate behaviour.
