Read the full story

Futile’s story begins in Latin: futilis meant “leaky.” A vessel that pours out easily, from 'fundere', to pour. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as futile. Latin 'futilis' literally meant easily poured out, like a leaky vessel, hence worthless. It reached English by 1550s as futile. 'futility' dates from the 1600s. Today, futile means pointless.
