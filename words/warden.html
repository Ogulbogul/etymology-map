Read the full story

Warden’s story begins in Frankish: wardon meant “to guard.” A Frankish word, related to 'ward' and 'guard'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as wardein. Norman French form; Parisian French said 'gardien'. It reached English by 1200s as warden. Prison wardens date from the 1500s. Today, warden means a person responsible for supervising a place.
