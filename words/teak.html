Read the full story

Teak’s story begins in Malayalam: tekka meant “teak tree.” The name of the durable tropical hardwood tree. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in Portuguese as teca. From Malayalam 'tēkku'. It reached English by 1600s-1700s as teak. British shipbuilders prized teak for warships in the 1800s. Today, teak means a durable, tropical hardwood used in furniture and shipbuilding.
