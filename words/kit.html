Read the full story

Kit’s story begins in Middle Dutch: kitte meant “wooden vessel.” A wooden tub or tankard bound with hoops. It reached English by 1300s as kit. Soldiers packed belongings in such a vessel, so the word came to mean their gear. Today, kit means a set of articles or equipment for a purpose.
