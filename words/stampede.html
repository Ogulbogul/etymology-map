Read the full story

Stampede’s story begins in Spanish: estampida meant “an uproar, crash.” Meaning "an uproar" or "crash". By 1800s, the word had taken hold in Mexican Spanish as estampida. Applied to a sudden mass flight of startled cattle. It reached English by 1800s as stampede. From Spanish 'estampar', to stamp; the Calgary Stampede began in 1912. Today, stampede means a sudden mass rush of frightened animals or panicked people.
