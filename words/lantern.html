Read the full story

Lantern’s story begins in Latin: lanterna meant “lamp.” From Greek 'lamptēr', torch, influenced by 'lucerna'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as lanterne. A protected lamp for carrying. It reached English by 1200s as lantern. The variant 'lanthorn' came from horn panels. Today, lantern means a lamp with a protective case.
