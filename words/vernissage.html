Read the full story

Vernissage’s story begins in French: vernis meant “varnish.” Varnish, since artists once varnished their paintings the day before an exhibition opened. It reached English by 1880s as vernissage. Adopted directly for the private preview opening of an art exhibition. Today, vernissage means a private preview of an art exhibition before it opens to the public.
