Read the full story

Coda’s story begins in Italian: coda meant “a tail.” Literally tail, the concluding passage of a musical piece. It reached English by 1770s as coda. Adopted directly for a concluding section, musical or otherwise. Today, coda means a concluding section of a piece of music, or the final part of an event.
