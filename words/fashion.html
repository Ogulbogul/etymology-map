Read the full story

Fashion’s story begins in Latin: facere meant “to make, to do.” The root of 'fact', 'factory' and 'feature'. Within Latin, it grew into factio by ancient. A making, a manner of making, from facere. From there it passed into Old French as facon by medieval. Carried into French for the way something is made or styled. It reached English by 1300s as fashion. Settled as the English word for a popular style, or to make something. Today, fashion means a popular style of dress or behavior at a given time, or to make or shape something.
