Giaour

3 stages from Persian to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means an old term, used historically by Turks, for a non-Muslim, especially a Christian

Where does the word "giaour" come from? It comes from Persian gabr / gaur, passing through Turkish before entering English (1560s).
