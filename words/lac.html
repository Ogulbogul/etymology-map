Lac

5 stages from Sanskrit to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means A resinous secretion of the lac insect, used to make shellac, varnish, and dye.

Where does the word "lac" come from? It comes from Sanskrit laksha, passing through Prakrit, Hindi and Persian before entering English (1600s).

laksha→lakkha→lakh→lak→lac
