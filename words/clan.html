Read the full story

Clan’s story begins in Latin: planta meant “sprout, offshoot.” Literally "sprout" or "offshoot", the root of a family line. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old Irish as cland. Came to mean one's children or descendants. From there it passed into Scottish Gaelic as clann by medieval. Used for a kin group descended from a common ancestor. It reached English by 1400s-1500s as clan. Borrowed to describe Scottish Highland kin groups. Today, clan means a group of close-knit, interrelated families, especially in Scotland.
