Read the full story

Village’s story begins in Latin: villa meant “country house.” A Roman country estate with its farm buildings. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as village. The cluster of dwellings that grew around an estate. It reached English by 1300s as village. 'villain' shares the root, originally a peasant from such a settlement. Today, village means a small community in a rural area.
