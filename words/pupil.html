Pupil

3 stages from Latin to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means the dark, circular opening in the center of the eye that regulates the amount of light entering

Where does the word "pupil" come from? It comes from Latin pupilla, passing through Old French before entering English (1400s).
