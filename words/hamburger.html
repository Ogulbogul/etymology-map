Read the full story

Hamburger’s story begins in German: Hamburg meant “the city of Hamburg.” The German port city known for a style of minced beef. Within German, it grew into Hamburger by 1800s. Described minced beef steak in the Hamburg style. It reached English by 1800s-1900s as hamburger. Brought to the US by German immigrants and reshaped into a sandwich. Today, hamburger means a cooked patty of ground meat, typically served in a bread bun.
