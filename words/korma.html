Read the full story

Korma’s story begins in Turkish: kavurma meant “roasted meat.” Roasted meat, verbal noun of kavurmak, to roast or fry. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Urdu as qorma. A Mughal court dish of meat braised in yoghurt and cream. It reached English by 1810s as korma. Hindi-Urdu 'qorma' names the braising technique itself rather than a particular sauce. Today, korma means a mild South Asian dish of meat or vegetables braised in a rich, creamy sauce.
