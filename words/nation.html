Read the full story

Nation’s story begins in Latin: nasci meant “to be born.” The root of 'native', 'nature' and 'natal'. Within Latin, it grew into natio by ancient. Birth, race, a people bound by common origin, from nasci (to be born). From there it passed into Old French as nacion by medieval. Carried into French for a people united under one government. It reached English by 1300s as nation. Settled as the English word for a large body of people under one government. Today, nation means a large body of people united by common descent, history, or government.
