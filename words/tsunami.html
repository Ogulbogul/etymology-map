Tsunami’s story begins in Japanese: tsunami meant “harbor wave.” Literally "harbor wave", named for the damage seen in coastal harbors. It reached English by 1896 as tsunami. Entered English reporting after the Sanriku earthquake and wave. Today, tsunami means a large, destructive ocean wave caused by an earthquake or other underwater disturbance.
