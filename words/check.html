Check

4 stages from Persian to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a written order to a bank to pay money; also, a mark of verification, or a stoppage or restraint

Where does the word "check" come from? It comes from Persian shah, passing through Vulgar Latin and Old French before entering English (c. 1300 - 1798).
