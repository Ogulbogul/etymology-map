Read the full story

Brackish’s story begins in Middle Dutch: brac meant “salty.” Salty, used for the mixed waters of the Low Countries' estuaries. It reached English by 1530s as brackish. Formed with the English ending '-ish'. Today, brackish means slightly salty, as water in estuaries.
