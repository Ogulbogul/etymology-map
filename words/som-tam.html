Read the full story

Som tam’s story begins in Thai: s̄̂mtảa meant “pounded sour salad.” Combining 'som' (sour) and 'tam' (pounded), naming a spicy salad made by pounding shredded green papaya. It reached English by late 1900s-2000s as som tam. Borrowed by food writers and menus as Thai cuisine spread internationally. Today, som tam means a spicy, tangy Thai salad made from pounded shredded green papaya.
