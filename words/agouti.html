Read the full story

Agouti’s story begins in Tupi: akuti meant “a forest rodent.” Named the large, rabbit-sized forest rodent. By 1600s, the word had taken hold in Portuguese/French as agouti. Adopted by Iberian and French colonists describing the animal. It reached English by 1600s as agouti. Borrowed into English natural history accounts. Today, agouti means a rabbit-sized, long-legged rodent native to Central and South America, also lending its name to a common animal coat coloring pattern.
