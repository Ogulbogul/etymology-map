Read the full story

Lozenge’s story begins in Gaulish: lausa meant “flat stone.” Possibly a Gaulish or Iberian word for a slab. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as losenge. Used in heraldry for a diamond-shaped figure. It reached English by 1300s as lozenge. Medicinal 'lozenges' were diamond-shaped tablets. Today, lozenge means a medicated sweet; a diamond shape.
