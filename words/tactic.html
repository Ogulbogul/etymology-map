Read the full story

Tactic’s story begins in Greek: taktikos meant “of arranging.” Greek, from 'tassein', to arrange troops. By 1600s, the word had taken hold in French as tactique. Came via Modern Latin 'tactica'. It reached English by 1600s as tactic. 'tactical' voting dates from the 1900s. Today, tactic means an action planned to achieve an end.
