Read the full story

Ipso facto’s story begins in Latin: ipso facto meant “by the fact itself.” By the fact itself, from ipse (itself) and factum (fact, deed). It reached English by 1500s as ipso facto. Adopted directly into English legal and philosophical writing. Today, ipso facto means by the fact itself, as an automatic and inevitable consequence.
