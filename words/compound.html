Compound’s story begins in Malay: kampung meant “a village, enclosure.” Meaning "a village" or "enclosure". It reached English by 1600s-1700s as compound. First recorded in 1679 in the records of the East India Company's Fort St George at Madras; probably picked up from Malay 'kampung' at the Company's early trading posts in the archipelago. Today, compound means a walled or fenced area enclosing a group of buildings.
