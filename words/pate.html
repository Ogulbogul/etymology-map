Read the full story

Pate’s story begins in Late Latin: pasta meant “paste, dough.” Paste, dough, the shared root behind pasta and pate. By medieval, the word had taken hold in French as pate. Settled in French for a savory paste of ground meat or liver. It reached English by 1706 as pate. Adopted directly for the rich, savory spreadable paste. Today, pate means a rich, savory paste made from ground meat, fish, or vegetables.
