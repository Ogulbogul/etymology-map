Read the full story

Recourse’s story begins in Latin: recurrere meant “to run back.” To run back, from re- (back) and currere (to run). Within Latin, it grew into recursus by ancient. A running back, from recurrere. From there it passed into Old French as recours by medieval. Carried into French for turning to something for help. It reached English by 1300s as recourse. Settled as the English word for a source of help in a difficult situation. Today, recourse means a source of help that can be turned to in a difficult situation.
