Read the full story

Mumbo jumbo’s story begins in Mandinka: maamajomboo meant “a masked enforcer figure in Mandinka ceremonies.” A costumed ancestral-spirit dancer summoned to settle village disputes. It reached English by 1700s-1800s as mumbo jumbo. European travelers' accounts mocked the ritual as baffling, shifting its sense to nonsense. Today, mumbo jumbo means confusing or meaningless language, ritual, or jargon intended to obscure rather than clarify.
