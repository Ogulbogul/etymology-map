Read the full story

Parasite’s story begins in Greek: parasitos meant “one who eats at another's table.” Greek, 'para', beside, plus 'sitos', food. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as parasite. A flatterer who dines at others' expense. It reached English by 1530s as parasite. The biological sense dates from the 1700s. Today, parasite means an organism living off another.
