Read the full story

Casino’s story begins in Italian: casa meant “house.” Meaning "house". By 1700s, the word had taken hold in Italian as casino. A diminutive meaning "a little house", used for social clubs and gambling houses. It reached English by 1700s-1800s as casino. Adopted directly from Italian. Today, casino means a public building or room used for gambling.
