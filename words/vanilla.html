Read the full story

Vanilla’s story begins in Spanish: vaina meant “pod, sheath.” Meaning "sheath" or "pod", from Latin vagina. By 1500s-1600s, the word had taken hold in Spanish as vainilla. A diminutive describing the slender seed pod. It reached English by 1600s as vanilla. Adopted from Spanish accounts of Mexican vanilla cultivation. Today, vanilla means a flavoring derived from the pods of a tropical orchid.
