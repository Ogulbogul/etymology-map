Read the full story

Dictionary’s story begins in Latin: dictio meant “a saying, a speaking.” A saying, a speaking, from dicere (to say). By medieval, the word had taken hold in Medieval Latin as dictionarium. Coined for a reference book of words and their meanings. It reached English by 1520s as dictionary. Settled as the English word for a reference book of words and definitions. Today, dictionary means a reference book listing words alphabetically along with their meanings.
