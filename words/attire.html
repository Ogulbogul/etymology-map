Read the full story

Attire’s story begins in Frankish: tieira meant “row, order.” A Frankish word for order or rank. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as atirier. 'a-' plus 'tire', order: to put in order. It reached English by 1300s as attire. The noun 'attire', clothing, followed. Today, attire means clothes, especially formal ones.
