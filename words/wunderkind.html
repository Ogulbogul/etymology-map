Read the full story

Wunderkind’s story begins in German: Wunder meant “wonder, marvel.” Wonder, marvel, combined with Kind (child). Within German, it grew into Wunderkind by 1800s. Wonder child, describing a child of extraordinary talent. It reached English by 1883 as wunderkind. Adopted directly for a young person of remarkable talent or success. Today, wunderkind means a young person who achieves great success or shows exceptional talent early in life.
