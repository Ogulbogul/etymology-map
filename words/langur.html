Read the full story

Langur’s story begins in Sanskrit: langula meant “tail.” Meant 'tail'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Hindi as langur. Named the long-tailed monkey. It reached English by 1820s as langur. British naturalists in India adopted the name. Today, langur means a long-tailed, leaf-eating monkey native to South and Southeast Asia.
