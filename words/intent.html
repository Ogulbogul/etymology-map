Read the full story

Intent’s story begins in Latin: intentus meant “stretched toward.” 'in-' plus 'tendere', to stretch. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as entent. A mind stretched toward a goal. It reached English by 1200s as intent. 'intent on' dates from the 1600s. Today, intent means intention; determined.
