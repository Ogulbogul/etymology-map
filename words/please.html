Read the full story

Please’s story begins in Latin: placere meant “to be agreeable.” The root of 'placid' and 'complacent'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as plaisir. The infinitive also became the noun 'pleasure'. It reached English by 1300s as please. The polite 'please' is short for 'if it please you'. Today, please means to make someone happy; used to make a polite request.
