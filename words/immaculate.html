Read the full story

Immaculate’s story begins in Latin: immaculatus meant “unstained.” 'in-', not, plus 'macula', spot. It reached English by 1400s as immaculate. The Immaculate Conception doctrine was defined in 1854. Today, immaculate means perfectly clean.
