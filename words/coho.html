Read the full story

Coho’s story begins in Halkomelem: k'ʷəxʷəθ meant “a species of Pacific salmon.” Named the mid-sized Pacific salmon species. It reached English by 1860s-1870s as coho. Borrowed into English by fishers and settlers on the Pacific coast. Today, coho means a species of Pacific salmon (Oncorhynchus kisutch), also called silver salmon.
