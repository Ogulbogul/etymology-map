Read the full story

Rickshaw’s story begins in Japanese: jinrikisha meant “a human-pulled cart.” Literally "human-powered vehicle" (jin "person" + riki "power" + sha "vehicle"). It reached English by 1800s as rickshaw. Shortened and adapted from the Japanese term, spreading widely in South Asia. Today, rickshaw means a small, two- or three-wheeled vehicle for carrying passengers, pulled or pedaled by a driver.
