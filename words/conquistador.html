Read the full story

Conquistador’s story begins in Latin: conquistare meant “to conquer.” To seek out thoroughly, to conquer, from com- (thoroughly) and quaerere (to seek). By 1500s, the word had taken hold in Spanish as conquistador. Settled in Spanish for the soldiers and explorers who conquered the Americas. It reached English by 1830s as conquistador. Adopted directly for the Spanish conquerors of the New World. Today, conquistador means a Spanish conqueror, especially one of those who conquered Mexico and Peru in the 1500s.
