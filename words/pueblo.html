Read the full story

Pueblo’s story begins in Latin: populus meant “a people, a population.” A people, a population. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Spanish as pueblo. Settled in Spanish for a town or village and its people. It reached English by 1800s as pueblo. Adopted directly for a village, especially the traditional settlements of the American southwest. Today, pueblo means a small town or village, especially a traditional settlement of the American southwest.
