Read the full story

Loess’s story begins in German: Löss meant “loose soil.” The geologist Karl Cäsar von Leonhard named the Rhine valley's loose silt from Alemannic 'lösch', loose. It reached English by 1830s as loess. Applied to vast deposits such as China's Loess Plateau. Today, loess means a fine windblown silt forming fertile soils.
