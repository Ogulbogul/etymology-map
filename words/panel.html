Read the full story

Panel’s story begins in Latin: pannus meant “piece of cloth.” The root of 'pane'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as panel. A list of jurors on parchment. It reached English by 1300s as panel. Discussion 'panels' date from the 1900s. Today, panel means a flat piece forming part of a surface; a group of experts.
