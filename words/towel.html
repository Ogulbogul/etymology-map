Read the full story

Towel’s story begins in Frankish: thwahlja meant “cloth for washing.” A Germanic word related to Old English 'þwean', to wash. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as toaille. A linen cloth for drying hands at table. It reached English by 1200s as towel. 'throw in the towel' is boxing slang from the early 1900s. Today, towel means a piece of absorbent cloth for drying oneself.
