Read the full story

Gochujang’s story begins in Korean: gochujang meant “fermented chili paste.” A fermented red chili paste central to Korean cooking. It reached English by 2000s as gochujang. Fermented in earthenware pots; chillies reached Korea from the Americas in the 1600s. Today, gochujang means a savory, spicy fermented Korean condiment made from red chili.
