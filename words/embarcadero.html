Read the full story

Embarcadero’s story begins in Spanish: embarcar meant “to embark.” To embark, from 'barca', boat. Within Spanish, it grew into embarcadero by 1500s. A landing place for boats. It reached English by 1840s as Embarcadero. San Francisco's waterfront road keeps the name. Today, embarcadero means a wharf or landing place.
