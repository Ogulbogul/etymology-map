Read the full story

Admire’s story begins in Latin: admirari meant “to wonder at.” 'ad-', at, plus 'mirari', to wonder; the root of 'miracle' and 'mirror'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as admirer. Used for astonishment before it meant approval. It reached English by 1590s as admire. First meant simply to be amazed, good or bad. Today, admire means to regard with respect or warm approval.
