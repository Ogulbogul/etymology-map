Read the full story

Lychee’s story begins in Chinese: lizhi meant “lychee fruit.” The name for the sweet, fragrant tropical fruit. It reached English by 1700s as lychee. Adopted through trade contact with China. Today, lychee means a small, sweet tropical fruit with a rough red skin and white flesh.
