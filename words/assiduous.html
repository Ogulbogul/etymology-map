Read the full story

Assiduous’s story begins in Latin: sedere meant “to sit.” To sit, giving rise to assidere (to sit down beside, attend to). By ancient, the word had taken hold in Latin as assiduus. Constantly sitting at one's task, diligent, from assidere. It reached English by 1530s as assiduous. Settled as the English word for showing great care and perseverance. Today, assiduous means showing great care, thoroughness, and perseverance in one's efforts.
