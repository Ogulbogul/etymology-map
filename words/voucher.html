Read the full story

Voucher’s story begins in Latin: vocare meant “to call, to summon.” To call, to summon. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Anglo-French as vocher. To call as a witness, to guarantee or vouch for something. It reached English by 1600s as voucher. Settled as the English word for a document entitling the holder to a good or service. Today, voucher means a document that entitles the holder to a discount or a specific good or service.
