Read the full story

Pastiche’s story begins in Italian: pasticcio meant “pie, medley.” A pie or medley, from Latin 'pasta', dough. By 1700s, the word had taken hold in French as pastiche. A painting or opera made up of borrowings. It reached English by 1870s as pastiche. Used for art and writing that imitates another style. Today, pastiche means a work imitating the style of another artist or period.
