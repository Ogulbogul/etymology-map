Read the full story

Contralto’s story begins in Italian: contralto meant “against the high voice.” Literally against-high, the lowest female vocal range. It reached English by 1730s as contralto. Italian 'contra', against, plus 'alto', the part sung against the high part. Today, contralto means the lowest female singing voice.
