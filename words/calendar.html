Read the full story

Calendar’s story begins in Latin: kalendae meant “first day of the month.” When interest on debts was due. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as kalendier. A register of dates. It reached English by 1200s as calendar. The Gregorian calendar was adopted in Britain in 1752. Today, calendar means a chart of days, weeks and months.
