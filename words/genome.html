Read the full story

Genome’s story begins in Greek: genos meant “birth, kind.” Birth, race, kind. By 1920, the word had taken hold in German as Genom. The botanist Hans Winkler blended 'Gen', gene, and 'Chromosom'. It reached English by 1920s as genome. The Human Genome Project sequenced the human genome between 1990 and 2003. Today, genome means the complete set of genes in an organism.
