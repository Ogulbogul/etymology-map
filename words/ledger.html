Read the full story

Ledger’s story begins in Middle Dutch: legger meant “a book that lies open.” One who lies, referring to a large book that lay permanently open on a desk for reference. It reached English by 1400s as ledger. Settled as the English word for a book of financial accounts. Today, ledger means a book or system recording financial transactions.
