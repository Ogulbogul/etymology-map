Read the full story

Creditor’s story begins in Latin: credere meant “to believe, to trust.” The root of 'credit', 'creed' and 'incredible'. Within Latin, it grew into creditor by ancient. One to whom something is entrusted, a lender, from credere. It reached English by 1400s as creditor. Settled as the English word for a person or institution to whom money is owed. Today, creditor means a person or institution to whom money is owed.
