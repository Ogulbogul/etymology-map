Read the full story

Parcel’s story begins in Vulgar Latin: particula meant “small part.” A diminutive of 'pars', part. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as parcelle. A portion of land or goods. It reached English by 1300s as parcel. A wrapped package dates from the 1600s. Today, parcel means a wrapped package; a piece of land.
