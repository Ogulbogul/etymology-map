Read the full story

Punish’s story begins in Latin: punire meant “to punish.” From 'poena', penalty, the root of 'pain' and 'penal'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as punir. Its stem 'puniss-' gave the English form. It reached English by 1300s as punish. 'punishing' for exhausting dates from the 1800s. Today, punish means to impose a penalty for an offence.
