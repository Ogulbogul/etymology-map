Read the full story

Plateau’s story begins in Old French: plat meant “flat.” By medieval, the word had taken hold in French as plateau. Came to mean a flat elevated area of land. It reached English by 1700s as plateau. Adopted directly from French, later used figuratively. Today, plateau means a flat highland area, or a period during which progress levels off.
