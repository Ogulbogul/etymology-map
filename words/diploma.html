Read the full story

Diploma’s story begins in Greek: diploma meant “a folded official document.” Literally a folded paper, an official document folded in two. It reached English by 1600s as diploma. Settled as the word for a certificate of achievement. Today, diploma means a certificate awarded for completing a course of study.
