Read the full story

Drama’s story begins in Greek: drama meant “an action, a deed.” Literally "action" or "a deed", from "dran" (to do). By ancient, the word had taken hold in Latin as drama. Adopted for a theatrical composition. It reached English by 1500s-1600s as drama. Settled as the English word for theatrical works. Today, drama means a play for theater, or an intense, emotional situation.
