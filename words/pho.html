Read the full story

Pho’s story begins in French: pot-au-feu meant “a beef and vegetable stew.” Literally "pot on the fire", a French beef and vegetable stew. By 1900s, the word had taken hold in Vietnamese as pho. Adapted during French colonial rule into a beef noodle soup. It reached English by 1900s-2000s as pho. Adopted as Vietnamese cuisine spread internationally. Today, pho means a Vietnamese soup of broth, rice noodles, herbs, and meat.
