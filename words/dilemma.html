Read the full story

Dilemma’s story begins in Greek: dilēmma meant “double proposition.” A 'double premise', a trap in argument from 'di-', two, and 'lēmma', assumption. By ancient, the word had taken hold in Late Latin as dilemma. A term of logic and rhetoric. It reached English by 1520s as dilemma. 'the horns of a dilemma' pictures the two choices as a bull's horns. Today, dilemma means a situation requiring a difficult choice between options.
