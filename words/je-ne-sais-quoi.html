Read the full story

Je ne sais quoi’s story begins in French: je ne sais quoi meant “I don't know what.” Literally I don't know what, describing an indefinable quality. It reached English by 1650s as je ne sais quoi. French 'I don't know what'; Dominique Bouhours wrote an essay on it in 1671. Today, je ne sais quoi means a pleasant quality that is hard to describe or identify.
