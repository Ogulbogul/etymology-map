Read the full story

Basis’s story begins in Greek: basis meant “step, pedestal.” From 'bainein', to step: that on which one steps. By ancient, the word had taken hold in Latin as basis. A pedestal or foundation of a column. It reached English by 1570s as basis. Used figuratively for the underlying principle. Today, basis means the underlying support or foundation for an idea.
