Read the full story

Mulligatawny’s story begins in Tamil: milagu thanni meant “pepper water.” Literally "pepper water", a spiced soup. It reached English by 1700s-1800s as mulligatawny. Adapted by the British in colonial India into a spiced soup. Today, mulligatawny means a spiced soup of South Indian origin, often made with meat and vegetables.
