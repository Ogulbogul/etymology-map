Read the full story

Pepper’s story begins in Sanskrit: pippali meant “pepper plant.” The name for the long pepper spice plant. By ancient, the word had taken hold in Greek as peperi. Greek traders bought it from India via the Red Sea; Alaric demanded pepper as ransom for Rome in 408. From there it passed into Latin as piper by ancient. Carried across the Roman spice trade. It reached English by medieval as pipor. Borrowed early into English from Latin. Today, pepper means a pungent spice made from the dried berries of a tropical vine.
