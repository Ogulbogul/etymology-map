Read the full story

Harass’s story begins in Old French: harer meant “to set a dog on.” An Old French hunting cry 'hare!'. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as harasser. To wear down with attacks. It reached English by 1610s as harass. 'harassment' at work dates from the 1900s. Today, harass means to subject to aggressive pressure.
