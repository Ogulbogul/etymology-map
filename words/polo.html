Read the full story

Polo’s story begins in Balti: pulu meant “a ball.” Literally ball, from the game played on horseback in the mountains of northern India. It reached English by 1870s as polo. Balti 'pulu', ball; British tea planters in Manipur formed the first polo club in 1859. Today, polo means a game played on horseback in which players use mallets to hit a ball.
