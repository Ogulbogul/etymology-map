Read the full story

Monopoly’s story begins in Greek: monopolion meant “the sole right to sell.” Literally sole selling, from monos (alone) and polein (to sell). It reached English by 1530s as monopoly. Settled as the English word for exclusive control of a market. Today, monopoly means exclusive control of the trade or supply of a product or service.
