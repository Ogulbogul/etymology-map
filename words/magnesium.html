Read the full story

Magnesium’s story begins in Greek: Magnesia meant “a region of Greece.” A district in Greece where a distinctive white mineral was found. It reached English by 1808 as magnesium. Coined by chemist Humphry Davy for the metal isolated from the Magnesian mineral. Today, magnesium means a light, silvery-white reactive metallic element.
