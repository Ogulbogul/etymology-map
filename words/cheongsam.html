Read the full story

Cheongsam’s story begins in Cantonese: chèuhngsāam meant “a fitted, high-collared traditional dress.” Literally 'long garment', naming a fitted, high-collared dress that became fashionable in Shanghai and Hong Kong. It reached English by 1950s-1960s as cheongsam. Cantonese 'long garment'; it became fashionable in 1920s Shanghai, where it is called 'qipao'. Today, cheongsam means a fitted, high-collared traditional Chinese dress, typically with side slits.
