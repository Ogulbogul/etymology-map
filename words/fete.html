Read the full story

Fete’s story begins in Latin: festus meant “festive, joyful.” An adjective meaning festive or joyful, giving the plural noun festa. By 1100s, the word had taken hold in Old French as feste. Kept the sense of a feast or celebration. From there it passed into French as fete by medieval. Narrowed but remained a general word for a festival or party. It reached English by 1754 as fete. Borrowed for an elaborate festival or party, popularized by Horace Walpole. Today, fete means a festival, celebration, or elaborate party.
