Realgar

3 stages from Arabic to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a soft, red mineral consisting of arsenic sulfide, historically used as a pigment

Where does the word "realgar" come from? It comes from Arabic rahj al-ghar, passing through Medieval Latin before entering English (1390s).
