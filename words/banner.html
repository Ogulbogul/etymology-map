Read the full story

Banner’s story begins in Gothic: bandwa meant “sign, signal.” A Germanic word for a sign or signal. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as banere. A lord's flag, which vassals rallied to in battle. It reached English by 1200s as banner. Later used for newspaper headlines and web advertising. Today, banner means a long strip of cloth with a slogan or design.
