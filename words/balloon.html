Read the full story

Balloon’s story begins in Italian: palla meant “ball.” A ball. Within Italian, it grew into pallone by 1500s. 'large ball', used in a ball game. From there it passed into French as ballon by 1500s. A large ball. It reached English by 1570s as balloon. The Montgolfier brothers' hot-air balloon of 1783 gave it its flying sense. Today, balloon means a bag inflated with air or gas.
