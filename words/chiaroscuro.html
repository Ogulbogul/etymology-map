Read the full story

Chiaroscuro’s story begins in Italian: chiaro meant “light and dark.” Light, bright, combined with oscuro (dark, obscure). It reached English by 1680s as chiaroscuro. Adopted directly for the artistic technique contrasting light and shadow. Today, chiaroscuro means the artistic use of strong contrasts between light and shadow.
