Read the full story

Modest’s story begins in Latin: modestus meant “keeping measure.” From 'modus', measure. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as modeste. Latin 'modestus' meant keeping due measure, from 'modus', measure, the same root as 'moderate'. It reached English by 1560s as modest. 'modest' income dates from the 1800s. Today, modest means unassuming about one's abilities.
