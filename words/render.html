Read the full story

Render’s story begins in Latin: reddere meant “to give back.” To give back, from re- (back) and dare (to give). By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as rendre. Carried into French for giving back or producing something. It reached English by 1300s as render. Settled as the English word for giving, producing, or representing something. Today, render means to provide or produce something, or to represent it in a particular form.
