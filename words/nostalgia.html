Read the full story

Nostalgia’s story begins in Greek: nostos meant “a homecoming.” A homecoming, combined with algos (pain) for the pain of homesickness. By 1688, the word had taken hold in New Latin as nostalgia. Coined by Swiss scholar Johannes Hofer at the University of Basel as a medical term for severe homesickness. It reached English by 1720s as nostalgia. Adopted from New Latin, later broadened to wistful longing for the past. Today, nostalgia means a wistful longing for the past.
