Read the full story

Chow mein’s story begins in Chinese: chao mian meant “fried noodles.” Literally "fried noodles". It reached English by 1900s as chow mein. Chinese 'stir-fried noodles', brought by Cantonese immigrants to the US. Today, chow mein means a Chinese dish of stir-fried noodles with vegetables and meat.
