Wampum

2 stages from Massachusett to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means traditional shell beads used by Native Americans as currency, ornamentation, or ceremony

Where does the word "wampum" come from? It comes from Massachusett wampumpeag, adopted directly into English (1600s).
