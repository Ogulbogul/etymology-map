Read the full story

Passion’s story begins in Late Latin: passio meant “suffering.” From 'pati', to suffer, the root of 'patient'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as passion. The Passion of Christ. It reached English by 1100s as passion. The sense 'strong emotion' dates from the 1500s. Today, passion means strong emotion; intense enthusiasm.
