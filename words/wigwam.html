Read the full story

Wigwam’s story begins in Abenaki: wikewam meant “a domed dwelling.” Meaning "their house", a domed dwelling of bent poles and bark. It reached English by 1600s as wigwam. Abenaki 'wikewam', dwelling; the word spread to other Native dwellings. Today, wigwam means a domed hut traditionally used by some Native American peoples.
