Read the full story

Pretty’s story begins in Old English: prættig meant “crafty.” 'cunning, crafty', from prætt 'trick, wile'. By 1400s, the word had taken hold in Middle English as prety. Softened to 'clever, skilful', then 'fine, pleasing', as of a pretty tale or trick. It reached English by 1500s as pretty. Settled on 'attractive in a delicate way'; the adverb, as in 'pretty good', is recorded from the 1560s. Today, pretty means attractive in a delicate way; to a fair degree.
