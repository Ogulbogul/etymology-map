Read the full story

Sever’s story begins in Latin: separare meant “to separate.” The root of 'separate'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as sevrer. French 'sevrer' now means to wean. It reached English by 1300s as sever. 'severance pay' dates from the 1900s. Today, sever means to cut off.
