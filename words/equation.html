Read the full story

Equation’s story begins in Latin: aequus meant “equal, even.” Equal, even, giving rise to aequare (to make equal). Within Latin, it grew into aequatio by ancient. An equalizing, a making equal, from aequare. It reached English by 1400s as equation. Settled as the English word for a mathematical statement showing two expressions are equal. Today, equation means a mathematical statement showing that two expressions are equal.
