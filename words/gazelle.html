Read the full story

Gazelle’s story begins in Arabic: ghazal meant “gazelle.” The Arabic name for the swift desert antelope. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as gazel. Adopted through trade and Crusader contact. It reached English by medieval as gazelle. Settled as the English name for the animal. Today, gazelle means a slender, swift antelope native to Africa and Asia.
