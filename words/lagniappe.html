Lagniappe

4 stages from Quechua to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a small, unexpected extra gift or benefit given along with a purchase, especially in the American South

Where does the word "lagniappe" come from? It comes from Quechua yapa, passing through Spanish and Louisiana French before entering English (1840s-1850s).
