Read the full story

Argue’s story begins in Late Latin: argutare meant “to prattle.” A frequentative of 'arguere', to make clear, prove. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as arguer. To put forward reasons. It reached English by 1300s as argue. The sense 'to quarrel' dates from the 1800s. Today, argue means to exchange opposing views.
