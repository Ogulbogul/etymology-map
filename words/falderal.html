Read the full story

Falderal’s story begins in English: fal-de-ral meant “song refrain.” A meaningless refrain in songs, like 'fa-la-la'. It reached English by 1820s as falderal. Came to mean a trifle, then nonsense; 'folderol' is a common variant. Today, falderal means nonsense; a showy but worthless trifle.
