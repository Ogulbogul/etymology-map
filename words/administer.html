Read the full story

Administer’s story begins in Latin: administrare meant “to serve, manage.” 'ad-' plus 'ministrare', to serve. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as aministrer. A learned borrowing. It reached English by 1300s as administer. 'administration' as government dates from the 1700s. Today, administer means to manage; to give a drug.
