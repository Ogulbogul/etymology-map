Read the full story

Prudent’s story begins in Latin: prudens meant “foreseeing.” Contracted from 'providens', foreseeing. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as prudent. Prudence was one of the four cardinal virtues of medieval teaching. It reached English by 1300s as prudent. 'prudence' was one of the four cardinal virtues. Today, prudent means acting with care for the future.
