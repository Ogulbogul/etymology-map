Read the full story

Caboose’s story begins in Middle Dutch: kabuis meant “ship's galley.” A ship's galley or cooking cabin. By 1740s, the word had taken hold in English as caboose. First a ship's galley. It reached English by 1850s as caboose. Transferred to the crew car at the rear of a freight train. Today, caboose means the crew's car at the end of a freight train.
