Read the full story

Spectrum’s story begins in Latin: spectrum meant “an appearance, an image.” An appearance, an image, from specere (to look at). It reached English by 1610s as spectrum. Adopted by physicist Isaac Newton for the band of colors produced by refracted light. Today, spectrum means a band of colors formed when light is refracted, or a broad range of related qualities.
