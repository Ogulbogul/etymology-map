Read the full story

Flak’s story begins in German: Fliegerabwehrkanone meant “anti-aircraft cannon.” An acronym meaning "aircraft defense cannon". Within German, it grew into Flak by 1930s-1940s. Shortened to the acronym for anti-aircraft fire. It reached English by 1940s as flak. Adopted during World War II, later used figuratively. Today, flak means anti-aircraft fire, or harsh criticism directed at someone.
