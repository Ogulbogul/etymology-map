Read the full story

Visit’s story begins in Latin: visitare meant “to go to see.” A frequentative of 'visere', to look at. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as visiter. Used for bishops inspecting parishes. It reached English by 1200s as visit. Originally God's 'visitation' of blessing or punishment. Today, visit means to go to see someone or somewhere.
