Read the full story

Galaxy’s story begins in Greek: galaxias meant “milky, relating to milk.” Literally milky, describing the Milky Way's pale band across the sky. It reached English by 1300s as galaxy. Settled as the word for the Milky Way, later any large star system. Today, galaxy means a large system of stars, gas, and dust bound by gravity.
