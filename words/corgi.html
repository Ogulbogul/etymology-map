Read the full story

Corgi’s story begins in Welsh: cor gi meant “dwarf dog.” Literally dwarf dog, from cor (dwarf) and ci (dog). It reached English by 1900s as corgi. Welsh 'cor', dwarf, plus 'ci', dog; Queen Elizabeth II kept more than 30 Pembroke corgis. Today, corgi means a short-legged Welsh breed of dog.
