Read the full story

Special’s story begins in Latin: specialis meant “individual, particular.” From 'species', kind. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as especial. 'especial' survives in 'especially'. It reached English by 1200s as special. 'special effects' dates from the 1900s. Today, special means better or different from usual.
