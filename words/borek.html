Read the full story

Borek’s story begins in Ottoman Turkish: börek meant “filled pastry.” Pies of paper-thin dough spread with Ottoman rule across the Balkans and Middle East. By 1600s, the word had taken hold in Bosnian as burek. In Bosnia 'burek' means only the meat-filled spiral; cheese or spinach versions are called 'pita'. It reached English by 1900s as borek. Entered English through Turkish, Balkan and Armenian cooking. Today, borek means a baked or fried pastry of thin dough with a savoury filling.
