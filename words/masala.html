Read the full story

Masala’s story begins in Arabic: maṣāliḥ meant “necessities.” Things needed, ingredients. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Urdu as masāla. Spices, ingredients; 'masala' films mix every genre. It reached English by 1780s as masala. 'chicken tikka masala' became a British favourite in the late 1900s. Today, masala means a mixture of ground spices used in Indian cooking.
