Read the full story

Gouache’s story begins in Italian: aquazzo meant “a water-based paint.” Possibly related to acqua (water), describing a water-based opaque paint. By 1700s, the word had taken hold in French as gouache. Settled in French for the opaque watercolor painting medium. It reached English by 1880s as gouache. Adopted directly for the opaque water-based paint medium. Today, gouache means an opaque watercolor paint, or a painting made with it.
