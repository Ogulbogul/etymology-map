Read the full story

Drill’s story begins in Middle Dutch: drillen meant “to bore, to turn.” Related to Dutch 'drillen', to turn or whirl. It reached English by 1610s as drill. Dutch military manuals also gave the sense of repetitive training. Today, drill means a tool for boring holes; repetitive practice.
