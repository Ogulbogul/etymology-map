Read the full story

Asylum’s story begins in Greek: asylon meant “sanctuary.” 'a-', not, plus 'sylē', right of seizure: a temple where fugitives could not be taken. By ancient, the word had taken hold in Latin as asylum. A place of refuge. It reached English by 1400s as asylum. Later an institution for the mentally ill; 'political asylum' dates from the 1950s. Today, asylum means protection granted to a refugee; a former term for a psychiatric hospital.
