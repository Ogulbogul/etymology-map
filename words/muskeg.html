Read the full story

Muskeg’s story begins in Cree: maskek meant “a swamp or bog.” A low-lying, waterlogged area of peat and moss found across the subarctic. It reached English by 1800s as muskeg. Cree 'maskek', swamp; covers large parts of northern Canada. Today, muskeg means a waterlogged, peat-forming bog found in subarctic and northern regions.
