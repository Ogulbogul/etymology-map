Democracy

4 stages from Greek to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a system of government in which power is held by the people through elections

Where does the word "democracy" come from? It comes from Greek demokratia, passing through Medieval Latin and Middle French before entering English (1500s-1600s).
