Rook

5 stages from Persian to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a chess piece, also called a castle, that moves horizontally or vertically

Where does the word "rook" come from? It comes from Persian rukh, passing through Arabic, Medieval Latin and Old French before entering English (1300s).
