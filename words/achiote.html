Achiote

3 stages from Nahuatl to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a red-orange seasoning and natural dye made from the seeds of the annatto shrub

Where does the word "achiote" come from? It comes from Nahuatl achiotl, passing through Spanish before entering English (1790s).
