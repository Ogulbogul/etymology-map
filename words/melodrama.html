Read the full story

Melodrama’s story begins in Greek: melos meant “a song.” A song, combined with French drame (drama). By 1770s, the word had taken hold in French as melodrame. Coined for a stage play combining spoken drama with music. It reached English by 1800s as melodrama. Broadened to mean a sensational, exaggerated dramatic style. Today, melodrama means a sensational dramatic story with exaggerated emotion, characters, or events.
