Read the full story

Tiffin’s story begins in English: tiff meant “a sip.” Dialect slang for a sip of liquor. By 1800s, the word had taken hold in Anglo-Indian as tiffing. British officials in India called their light midday meal 'tiffing'. It reached English by 1800s as tiffin. Mumbai's 'dabbawalas' still deliver home-cooked lunches in stacked tiffin tins. Today, tiffin means a light meal, especially lunch, in India.
