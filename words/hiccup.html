Read the full story

Hiccup’s story begins in English: hicket meant “spasm of the diaphragm.” An imitation of the sound, like French hoquet and Dutch hik. It reached English by 1600s as hiccup. The alternative spelling 'hiccough' appeared in the 1620s through a false link with 'cough', though the pronunciation never changed. Today, hiccup means an involuntary spasm of the diaphragm; a minor setback.
