Read the full story

Connoisseur’s story begins in Latin: cognoscere meant “to know.” Meaning "to know" or "to learn". By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as conoistre. Carried the meaning of knowing or recognizing. From there it passed into French as connoisseur by 1700s. Came to mean an expert judge of taste. It reached English by 1700s as connoisseur. Adopted directly from French. Today, connoisseur means an expert judge of quality, especially in the fine arts, food, or drink.
