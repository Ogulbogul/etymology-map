Read the full story

Format’s story begins in Latin: forma meant “shape, mold, appearance.” Shape, mold, appearance. Within Latin, it grew into formatus by ancient. Shaped, given a form, past participle of formare. From there it passed into German as format by 1800s. Adopted for the size and shape of a printed book. It reached English by 1840 as format. Settled as the English word for the way something is arranged or presented. Today, format means the way in which something is arranged, organized, or presented.
