Read the full story

Gozleme’s story begins in Turkish: gözleme meant “filled flatbread.” Rolled thin by village women and cooked on the domed iron 'sac' griddle. It reached English by 1990s as gozleme. A fixture of Australian markets, brought by Turkish immigrants who arrived under a 1967 migration agreement. Today, gozleme means a Turkish flatbread folded around a filling and cooked on a griddle.
