Read the full story

Robot’s story begins in Czech: robota meant “forced labor, drudgery.” Coined by Karel Čapek for his play R.U.R., meaning forced labor. It reached English by 1920s as robot. From Karel Čapek's 1920 play 'R.U.R.'; his brother Josef suggested the word, from Czech 'robota', forced labour. Today, robot means a machine capable of automatically carrying out physical tasks.
