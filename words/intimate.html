Read the full story

Intimate’s story begins in Latin: intimus meant “innermost.” Superlative of 'intus', within. By ancient, the word had taken hold in Late Latin as intimare. Meant to put into, then to make known publicly. It reached English by 1630s as intimate. Both 'intimate' as close and 'to intimate', to hint, come from this. Today, intimate means closely acquainted; to hint.
