Read the full story

Pump’s story begins in Middle Dutch: pompe meant “pipe, ship's pump.” Probably imitative of the sound of pumping; Dutch ships needed them constantly to clear bilge water. By 1400s, the word had taken hold in Middle English as pumpe. First recorded as a ship's pump. It reached English by 1600s as pump. The verb 'pump someone for information' dates from the 1600s. Today, pump means a device that forces liquid or gas to move; to move with such a device.
