Read the full story

Envelope’s story begins in Old French: envelopper meant “to wrap, enclose.” Meaning "to wrap up" or "enclose". By 1600s, the word had taken hold in French as enveloppe. The noun form for a wrapping or covering. It reached English by 1700s as envelope. Adopted for the paper wrapping used for letters. Today, envelope means a flat paper container used to enclose a letter or document.
