Read the full story

Cashmere’s story begins in Sanskrit/Kashmiri: Kashmir meant “the region of Kashmir.” The Himalayan region famous for the fine wool of its mountain goats. It reached English by 1800s as cashmere. Kashmir shawls brought back by East India Company officers became a European fashion around 1800. Today, cashmere means fine soft wool from the Kashmir goat, or fabric made from it.
