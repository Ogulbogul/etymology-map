Read the full story

Dormant’s story begins in Latin: dormire meant “to sleep.” The root of 'dormitory'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as dormant. Present participle of 'dormir'. It reached English by 1300s as dormant. Heraldic lions 'dormant' are shown asleep. Today, dormant means temporarily inactive.
