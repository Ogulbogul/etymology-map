Read the full story

Mousse’s story begins in French: mousse meant “froth, moss.” Meaning "froth" or "moss". It reached English by 1900s as mousse. Adopted for a light, airy dessert or styling product. Today, mousse means a light, airy dish made by whipping ingredients, or a similar hair product.
