Read the full story

Nachos’s story begins in Mexican Spanish: Nacho meant “Nacho (a nickname).” The nickname of Ignacio Anaya, who invented the snack at a restaurant in Piedras Negras for visiting American officers' wives. It reached English by 1950s as nachos. Spread through Texas and became a stadium food in the 1970s. Today, nachos means tortilla chips topped with melted cheese and chillies.
