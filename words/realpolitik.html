Read the full story

Realpolitik’s story begins in German: Realpolitik meant “practical politics.” Coined by the writer Ludwig von Rochau and associated with Bismarck's statecraft. It reached English by 1910s as realpolitik. Borrowed during the First World War era. Today, realpolitik means politics based on practical rather than moral considerations.
