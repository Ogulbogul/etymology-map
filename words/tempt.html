Read the full story

Tempt’s story begins in Latin: temptare meant “to test, try.” The root of 'attempt'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as tempter. The Devil tempting Christ. It reached English by 1200s as tempt. 'temptation' followed. Today, tempt means to entice to do something unwise.
