Read the full story

Degrade’s story begins in Medieval Latin: degradare meant “to step down, to reduce rank.” To step down, to reduce in rank, from de- (down) and gradus (a step). It reached English by 1400s as degrade. Settled as the English word for lowering someone's status, or reducing quality. Today, degrade means to lower someone in status or dignity, or to reduce the quality of something.
