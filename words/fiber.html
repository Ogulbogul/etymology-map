Read the full story

Fiber’s story begins in Latin: fibra meant “filament.” Possibly related to 'filum', thread. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as fibre. A learned borrowing. It reached English by 1540s as fiber. Dietary 'fibre' dates from the 1970s. Today, fiber means a thread or filament.
