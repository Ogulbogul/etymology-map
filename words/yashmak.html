Read the full story

Yashmak’s story begins in Proto-Turkic: yaĺ- meant “to cover.” Root meaning "to cover, close". By medieval, the word had taken hold in Turkish as yaşmak. Developed into the name for a face veil covering all but the eyes. It reached English by 1835-45 as yashmak. Entered English through descriptions of Ottoman women's dress. Today, yashmak means a veil covering the lower face, traditionally worn by Muslim women in Turkey.
