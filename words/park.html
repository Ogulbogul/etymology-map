Read the full story

Park’s story begins in Medieval Latin: parricus meant “enclosure.” A Medieval Latin word of Germanic origin, related to 'paddock'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as parc. An enclosed hunting ground. It reached English by 1200s as park. 'car park' dates from the 1920s. Today, park means a public green area; to leave a vehicle.
