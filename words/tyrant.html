Read the full story

Tyrant’s story begins in Greek: tyrannos meant “absolute ruler.” A ruler who seized power outside tradition; some, like Peisistratos of Athens, were popular. By ancient, the word had taken hold in Latin as tyrannus. Took on the sense of a cruel ruler. It reached English by 1200s as tyrant. The 't' ending came from French 'tyrant'. Today, tyrant means a cruel and oppressive ruler.
