Read the full story

Email’s story begins in American English: electronic mail meant “computer-to-computer messages.” Ray Tomlinson at BBN in Cambridge, Massachusetts, sent the first email between networked computers on ARPANET in 1971 and chose the @ sign for addresses. It reached English by 1980s as email. Shortened to e-mail by the early 1980s; the Associated Press style guide dropped the hyphen in 2011. Today, email means messages sent electronically from one computer user to another.
