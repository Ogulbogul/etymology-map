Read the full story

Criminology’s story begins in Latin: crimen meant “a crime, an accusation.” A crime, an accusation. It reached English by 1890 as criminology. Coined by combining Latin crimen with Greek logos for the scientific study of crime. Today, criminology means the scientific study of crime, criminals, and the criminal justice system.
