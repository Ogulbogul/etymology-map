Seersucker

3 stages from Persian to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a thin, puckered cotton fabric often used for summer clothing

Where does the word "seersucker" come from? It comes from Persian shir o shakar, passing through Hindi before entering English (1720s).

shir o shakar→sirsakar→seersucker
