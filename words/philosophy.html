Read the full story

Philosophy’s story begins in Greek: philosophia meant “love of wisdom.” Literally "love of wisdom". By ancient, the word had taken hold in Latin as philosophia. Adopted through Greek influence on Roman education. From there it passed into Old French as filosofie by medieval. Carried the discipline's name into French. It reached English by medieval as philosophy. Settled as the English word for the discipline. Today, philosophy means the study of fundamental questions about existence, knowledge, and ethics.
