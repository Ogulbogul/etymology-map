Read the full story

Monk’s story begins in Greek: monakhos meant “solitary.” Greek, from 'monos', alone. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old English as munuc. Came via Late Latin 'monachus'. It reached English by 1200s as monk. 'monastery' shares the root. Today, monk means a member of a religious community of men.
