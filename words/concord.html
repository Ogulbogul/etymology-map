Read the full story

Concord’s story begins in Latin: cor meant “heart.” Heart, combined with com- (together). Within Latin, it grew into concordia by ancient. Agreement, harmony, hearts together, from com- and cor. From there it passed into Old French as concorde by medieval. Carried into French for a state of peaceful agreement. It reached English by 1300s as concord. Settled as the English word for a state of peaceful agreement and harmony. Today, concord means a state of peaceful agreement and harmony between people or groups.
