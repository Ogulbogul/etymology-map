Read the full story

Scholar’s story begins in Late Latin: schola meant “school.” From Greek 'skholē', leisure spent in learning. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as escoler. Medieval students were 'scholars' at cathedral schools. It reached English by 1300s as scholar. Later a learned person rather than a pupil. Today, scholar means a learned person; a student holding a scholarship.
