Read the full story

Fountain’s story begins in Late Latin: fontana meant “spring.” From 'fons', spring, the root of 'font'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as fontaine. A natural spring. It reached English by 1400s as fountain. Ornamental fountains spread in the Renaissance. Today, fountain means an ornamental jet of water.
