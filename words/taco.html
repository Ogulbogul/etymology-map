Read the full story

Taco’s story begins in Mexican Spanish: taco meant “a filled, folded tortilla.” Possibly from a word for a plug or wad, describing a filled, folded tortilla. It reached English by 1940s as taco. Mexican silver miners used 'taco' for gunpowder wrapped in paper, a possible origin. Today, taco means a folded or rolled tortilla filled with meat, beans, or vegetables.
