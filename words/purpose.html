Read the full story

Purpose’s story begins in Latin: proponere meant “to put forward.” 'pro-', forth, plus 'ponere', to place, the root of 'propose'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as porpos. What one sets forth to do. It reached English by 1200s as purpose. 'on purpose' dates from the 1500s. Today, purpose means the reason for which something is done.
