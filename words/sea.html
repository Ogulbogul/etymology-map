Read the full story

Sea’s story begins in Proto-Germanic: saiwiz meant “sea, lake.” Of unknown origin, perhaps a pre-Germanic word from the North Sea coast. By 700s-1000s, the word had taken hold in Old English as sǣ. Used for both seas and lakes, as German 'See' still is. It reached English by 1500s as sea. 'at sea', meaning confused, dates from the 1700s. Today, sea means the expanse of salt water covering most of the Earth.
