Read the full story

Refine’s story begins in Latin: re- + fin meant “make fine again.” 're-' plus 'fine', from Latin 'finis', end or perfection. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as raffiner. Used of sugar and metals. It reached English by 1580s as refine. Oil 'refineries' date from the 1800s. Today, refine means to remove impurities; to improve.
