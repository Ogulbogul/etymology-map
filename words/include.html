Read the full story

Include’s story begins in Latin: includere meant “to close in, to shut in.” To close in, to shut in, from in- (in) and claudere (to close). It reached English by 1400s as include. Settled as the English word for containing something as part of a whole. Today, include means to contain something as a part of a whole, or to add it to a group.
