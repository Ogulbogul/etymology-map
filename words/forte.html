Read the full story

Forte’s story begins in Italian: forte meant “strong, loud.” Literally strong, loud, adopted as a musical dynamic marking. It reached English by 1700s as forte. Adopted for loud musical passages, and separately for a personal strength. Today, forte means played loudly in music, or a thing at which a person excels.
