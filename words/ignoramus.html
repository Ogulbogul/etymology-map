Read the full story

Ignoramus’s story begins in Latin: ignōrāmus meant “we do not know.” 'we do not know', from 'ignōrāre'. It reached English by 1610s as ignoramus. Grand juries wrote it on indictments they rejected; George Ruggle's 1615 play 'Ignoramus' made it the name of a foolish lawyer. Today, ignoramus means an ignorant or stupid person.
