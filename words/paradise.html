Paradise

5 stages from Old Persian to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a place or state of perfect happiness; heaven

Where does the word "paradise" come from? It comes from Old Persian pairidaēza, passing through Greek, Latin and Old French before entering English (c. 1200s).
