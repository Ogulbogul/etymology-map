Read the full story

Falsetto’s story begins in Italian: falsetto meant “a small false voice.” A diminutive of falso (false), a high artificial voice above a singer's natural range. It reached English by 1770s as falsetto. Adopted directly for the artificially high vocal register. Today, falsetto means a high-pitched vocal register used above a singer's normal range.
