Read the full story

Alert’s story begins in Italian: all'erta meant “on the watch.” Italian 'erta', a lookout on high ground, from 'erigere', to raise. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as alerte. A military cry to watch out. It reached English by 1590s as alert. 'red alert' dates from the 1940s. Today, alert means quick to notice danger; a warning.
