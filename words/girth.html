Read the full story

Girth’s story begins in Old Norse: gjörth meant “a belt, an encircling band.” A belt, a band that encircles something. It reached English by 1300s as girth. Settled as the English word for the measurement around something. Today, girth means the measurement around the middle of something, or a band encircling it.
