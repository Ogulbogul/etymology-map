Khedive

5 stages from Old Persian to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means the title of the viceroy of Egypt under nominal Ottoman rule from 1867 to 1914

Where does the word "khedive" come from? It comes from Old Persian khvadata-, passing through Persian, Turkish and French before entering English (1867).
