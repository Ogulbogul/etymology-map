Read the full story

Chromium’s story begins in Greek: chroma meant “color.” Color, since the element's compounds display vivid colors. It reached English by 1797 as chromium. Coined by its discoverer for the element known for its colorful compounds. Today, chromium means a hard, shiny, corrosion-resistant metallic element used in alloys and plating.
