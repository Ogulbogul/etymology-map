Read the full story

Vocation’s story begins in Latin: vocare meant “to call.” To call. By ancient, the word had taken hold in Latin as vocatio. A calling, a summons, from vocare. It reached English by 1400s as vocation. Settled as the English word for a strong feeling of suitability for a particular career. Today, vocation means a strong sense of suitability or calling for a particular occupation.
