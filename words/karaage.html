Read the full story

Karaage’s story begins in Japanese: karaage meant “food lightly coated and deep-fried.” Literally 'Chinese-style frying', naming lightly floured deep-fried food adapted from a continental technique. It reached English by 2010s as karaage. A cooking method influenced by Chinese frying techniques. Today, karaage means japanese-style deep-fried chicken, lightly marinated and coated before frying.
