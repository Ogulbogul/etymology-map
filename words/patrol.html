Read the full story

Patrol’s story begins in French: patouiller meant “to paddle in mud.” From 'patte', paw. Within French, it grew into patrouille by 1600s. Soldiers tramping through the mud. It reached English by 1660s as patrol. Police 'patrol' cars date from the 1900s. Today, patrol means a person or group keeping watch.
