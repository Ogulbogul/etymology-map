Read the full story

Waffle’s story begins in Dutch: wafel meant “a gridded batter cake.” The Dutch word for the gridded batter cake. It reached English by 1700s as waffle. Adopted directly from Dutch. Today, waffle means a crisp batter cake with a grid pattern, cooked between two hinged plates.
