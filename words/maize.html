Read the full story

Maize’s story begins in Taíno: mahiz meant “corn plant.” The Taíno name for the corn plant. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in Spanish as maíz. Adopted after Spanish contact with the Caribbean. It reached English by 1500s as maize. Borrowed from Spanish for the New World grain. Today, maize means a cereal plant with large grains on a cob, also called corn.
