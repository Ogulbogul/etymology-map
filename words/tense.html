Read the full story

Tense’s story begins in Latin: tempus meant “time.” The root of 'temporary'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as tens. Used by grammarians for verb time. It reached English by 1300s as tense. 'tense' meaning stretched is a separate word from Latin 'tensus'. Today, tense means the form of a verb showing time; stretched tight.
