Read the full story

Wish’s story begins in Proto-Germanic: wunskijaną meant “to wish.” From the ancient root for desire that also gave Latin 'Venus' and English 'win'. By 700s-1000s, the word had taken hold in Old English as wȳscan. German 'wünschen' and Dutch 'wensen' are its cousins. It reached English by 1500s as wish. 'wishbone' dates from the 1850s, from pulling a fowl's forked bone to make a wish. Today, wish means to want something to happen.
