Bureaucracy

3 stages from French to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a system of government administered by officials and departments

Where does the word "bureaucracy" come from? It comes from French bureau, passing through French before entering English (1800s).

bureau→bureaucratie→bureaucracy
