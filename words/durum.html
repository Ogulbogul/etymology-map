Read the full story

Durum’s story begins in Turkish: dürüm meant “a roll, wrap.” From 'dürmek', to roll up. By 1990s, the word had taken hold in German as Dürüm. Became a staple of Turkish snack bars in Germany alongside the doner sandwich. It reached English by 2000s as durum. Spread through British and European kebab shops. Today, durum means a flatbread wrap rolled around doner or grilled meat.
