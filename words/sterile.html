Read the full story

Sterile’s story begins in Latin: sterilis meant “barren.” Related to Greek 'steira', barren cow. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as sterile. A learned borrowing. It reached English by 1550s as sterile. 'sterilized' medical tools date from the 1800s. Today, sterile means free from bacteria; unable to reproduce.
