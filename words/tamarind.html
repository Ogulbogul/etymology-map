Read the full story

Tamarind’s story begins in Arabic: tamr hindi meant “the Indian date.” Literally the Indian date, describing the sweet-sour fruit pod. It reached English by 1500s as tamarind. Settled as the word for the tangy tropical fruit. Today, tamarind means a tropical fruit with a tangy, sweet-sour pulp used in cooking.
