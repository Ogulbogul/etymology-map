Read the full story

Bowsprit’s story begins in Middle Low German: boegspriet meant “bow pole.” 'boeg', bow of a ship, plus 'spriet', pole. It reached English by 1300s as bowsprit. Used for the spar projecting forward from a ship's bow. Today, bowsprit means a spar projecting from the bow of a sailing ship.
