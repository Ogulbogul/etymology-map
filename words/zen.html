Read the full story

Zen’s story begins in Sanskrit: dhyana meant “meditation.” Meaning "meditation". By medieval, the word had taken hold in Chinese as chan. Adopted through the spread of Buddhism into China. From there it passed into Japanese as zen by medieval. Adopted through the spread of Buddhism from China to Japan. It reached English by 1900s as zen. Adopted as Zen Buddhism became known in the West. Today, zen means a school of Buddhism emphasizing meditation, or a state of calm and focus.
