Read the full story

Omniscient’s story begins in Latin: omnis meant “all, every.” All, every, combined with sciens (knowing, from scire, to know). By medieval, the word had taken hold in Medieval Latin as omnisciens. All-knowing, formed from omnis (all) and sciens (knowing). It reached English by 1600s as omniscient. Settled as the English word for having infinite knowledge. Today, omniscient means knowing everything, having infinite awareness and understanding.
