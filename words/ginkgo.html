Ginkgo

3 stages from Chinese to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means an ancient species of tree with distinctive fan-shaped leaves

Where does the word "ginkgo" come from? It comes from Chinese yin xing, passing through Japanese before entering English (1700s).
