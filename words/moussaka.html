Moussaka

4 stages from Arabic to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a baked Greek and Middle Eastern dish of layered eggplant, meat, and bechamel sauce

Where does the word "moussaka" come from? It comes from Arabic musaqqa'a, passing through Ottoman Turkish and Greek before entering English (1900s).
