Read the full story

Corporal’s story begins in Latin: corpus meant “body.” The root of 'corpse', 'corporation' and 'corps'. Within Latin, it grew into corporalis by ancient. Of the body, from corpus. It reached English by 1400s as corporal. Settled as the English word for something relating to the physical body, and separately a military rank. Today, corporal means relating to the physical body, or a military rank above private.
