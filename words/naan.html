Read the full story

Naan’s story begins in Persian: nan meant “bread.” The general Persian word for bread. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Hindi/Urdu as naan. Applied to the leavened flatbread baked in a tandoor. It reached English by 1900s as naan. Baked on the wall of a tandoor clay oven; a Persian word recorded by Amir Khusrau in the 1300s. Today, naan means a soft, leavened flatbread traditionally baked in a tandoor oven.
