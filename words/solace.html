Read the full story

Solace’s story begins in Latin: solari meant “to comfort, to soothe.” The root of 'console' and 'inconsolable'. Within Latin, it grew into solacium by ancient. Comfort, relief, from solari. From there it passed into Old French as solas by medieval. Carried into French for a feeling of comfort in sorrow. It reached English by 1300s as solace. Settled as the English word for comfort in a time of distress. Today, solace means comfort or relief experienced during a time of sadness or distress.
