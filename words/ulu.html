Read the full story

Ulu’s story begins in Inuktitut: ulu meant “a woman's knife.” Named the crescent-shaped all-purpose knife traditionally used by women. It reached English by 1900s as ulu. Inuit women's knife used for skinning and cutting. Today, ulu means a traditional crescent-shaped, all-purpose knife historically used by Inuit and other Arctic peoples.
