Read the full story

Fiancee’s story begins in Latin: fidare meant “to trust.” By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as fiancer. Meaning "to promise" or "betroth". From there it passed into French as fiancée by 1800s. The feminine form for a woman engaged to be married. It reached English by 1800s as fiancee. From French 'fiancer', to promise, from Old French 'fiance', a pledge. Today, fiancee means a woman who is engaged to be married.
