Read the full story

Darbuka’s story begins in Arabic: darabukka meant “drum.” From 'daraba', to strike. By 1800s, the word had taken hold in Turkish as darbuka. Used in Turkish, Roma and belly-dance music. It reached English by 1900s as darbuka. Entered English with Middle Eastern music; also spelled 'darabukka' and 'doumbek'. Today, darbuka means a goblet-shaped hand drum of the Middle East and Turkey.
