Read the full story

Forlorn hope’s story begins in Dutch: verloren hoop meant “lost troop.” 'lost troop', hoop meaning a band or heap: the advance party of soldiers sent first into a breach, expected to die. It reached English by 1570s as forlorn hope. English soldiers in the Dutch wars heard hoop as 'hope'; Wellington's storming parties at Badajoz in 1812 were still called the forlorn hope. Today, forlorn hope means a desperate or hopeless undertaking.
