Read the full story

Chaparral’s story begins in Basque: txapar meant “thicket.” A thicket. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Spanish as chaparro. A dwarf evergreen oak. It reached English by 1850s as chaparral. Used for California's scrub-covered hills. Today, chaparral means dense thorny shrubland, especially in California.
