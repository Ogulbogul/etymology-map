Read the full story

Classic’s story begins in Latin: classicus meant “of the first class.” From 'classis', a class of Roman citizens. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as classique. A learned borrowing. It reached English by 1610s as classic. 'classic' cars date from the 1900s. Today, classic means judged over time to be of the highest quality.
