Read the full story

Capacity’s story begins in Latin: capere meant “to take, to hold.” To take, to hold. Within Latin, it grew into capacitas by ancient. The ability to hold much, from capax (able to hold). It reached English by 1400s as capacity. Settled as the English word for the maximum amount something can contain or produce. Today, capacity means the maximum amount that something can contain, hold, or produce.
