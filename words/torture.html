Read the full story

Torture’s story begins in Latin: torquere meant “to twist.” To twist. By ancient, the word had taken hold in Latin as tortura. A twisting, torment, from torquere. From there it passed into French as torture by 1400s. Carried into French for the deliberate infliction of severe pain. It reached English by 1500s as torture. Settled as the English word for the deliberate infliction of severe pain. Today, torture means the deliberate infliction of severe pain as punishment or coercion.
