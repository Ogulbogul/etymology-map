Read the full story

Computer’s story begins in Latin: computare meant “to calculate.” To calculate, to sum up, from com- (together) and putare (to reckon). It reached English by 1640s as computer. Originally meant a person who calculates, later a machine performing calculations. Today, computer means an electronic machine that processes data and performs calculations.
