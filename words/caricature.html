Read the full story

Caricature’s story begins in Italian: caricare meant “to load.” To load, overload. Within Italian, it grew into caricatura by 1600s. 'an overloading'; the Bolognese painter Annibale Carracci is credited with the art of exaggerated portraits. It reached English by 1740s as caricature. Came via French; William Hogarth and James Gillray made it a British art. Today, caricature means a picture exaggerating a person's features for comic effect.
