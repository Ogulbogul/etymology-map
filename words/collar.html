Read the full story

Collar’s story begins in Latin: collum meant “neck.” The root of 'accolade'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as coler. A piece of armour for the neck. It reached English by 1300s as collar. 'white-collar' and 'blue-collar' date from the 1920s. Today, collar means the band around the neck of a garment.
