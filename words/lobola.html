Read the full story

Lobola’s story begins in Zulu: ukulobola meant “to give the bride price.” Described the customary transfer of cattle or goods to a bride's family. It reached English by 1897 as lobola. Traditionally paid in cattle, though cash is now common. Today, lobola means a bride price or dowry paid by a groom's family to the bride's family, in southern African custom.
