Knickerbocker’s story begins in Dutch: Knickerbocker meant “a surname.” A Dutch family name in New Netherland. By 1809, the word had taken hold in American English as Knickerbocker. Washington Irving's comic 'History of New York' by 'Diedrich Knickerbocker' made it a name for Dutch New Yorkers. It reached English by 1850s as knickerbockers. Named after the knee breeches in the book's illustrations; shortened to 'knickers' in the 1860s. Today, knickerbocker means loose breeches gathered at the knee.
