Read the full story

Turkmen’s story begins in Turkic: Türkmen meant “Turkmen.” Used for the Oghuz nomads who converted to Islam. It reached English by 1600s as Turcoman. English traders and travellers used 'Turcoman'; 'Turkoman carpets' became prized in the West. Today, turkmen means a member of a Turkic people of Central Asia, mostly in Turkmenistan.
