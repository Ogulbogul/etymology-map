Read the full story

Yurt’s story begins in Turkic: yurt meant “a nomad's dwelling place.” A round, portable felt-covered tent used by nomadic peoples of Central Asia. It reached English by 1780s as yurt. Adopted directly by European travelers describing Central Asian nomadic life. Today, yurt means a round, portable tent traditionally used by nomads of Central Asia.
