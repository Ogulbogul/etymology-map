Sugar

5 stages from Sanskrit to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a sweet crystalline substance obtained from sugarcane or sugar beet

Where does the word "sugar" come from? It comes from Sanskrit śarkarā, passing through Persian, Arabic and Old French before entering English (c. 1300s).
