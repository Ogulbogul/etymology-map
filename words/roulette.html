Read the full story

Roulette’s story begins in Latin: rotella meant “a small wheel.” A small wheel, a diminutive of rota (wheel). By 1700s, the word had taken hold in French as roulette. Settled in French for the spinning wheel game of chance. It reached English by 1745 as roulette. Adopted directly for the casino game played with a spinning wheel. Today, roulette means a casino gambling game in which a ball is spun around a numbered wheel.
