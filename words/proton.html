Read the full story

Proton’s story begins in Greek: protos meant “first.” First, describing the first identified positively charged subatomic particle. It reached English by 1920 as proton. Coined by physicist Ernest Rutherford for the positively charged nuclear particle. Today, proton means a stable subatomic particle with a positive electric charge, found in atomic nuclei.
