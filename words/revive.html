Read the full story

Revive’s story begins in Latin: vivere meant “to live.” To live, combined with re- (again). Within Latin, it grew into revivere by ancient. To live again, from re- (again) and vivere (to live). It reached English by 1400s as revive. Settled as the English word for restoring something to life or activity. Today, revive means to restore someone or something to life, activity, or good health.
