Patchouli

2 stages from Tamil to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means A bushy herb of the mint family, and the heavy, musky essential oil made from its leaves.

Where does the word "patchouli" come from? It comes from Tamil pachchai ilai, adopted directly into English (1845).
