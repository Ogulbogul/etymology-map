Read the full story

Limousine’s story begins in French: Limousin meant “Limousin region.” A region of central France around Limoges. Within French, it grew into limousine by 1800s. A hooded cloak worn by Limousin shepherds, then a car whose driver sat outside under a similar hood. It reached English by 1902 as limousine. Borrowed with the early motor car. Today, limousine means a large, luxurious car, often driven by a chauffeur.
