Read the full story

Bolero’s story begins in Spanish: bolero meant “dance.” A dance said to have been created around 1780 by the dancer Sebastián Cerezo. It reached English by 1780s as bolero. Ravel's 'Boléro' of 1928 made the rhythm world-famous; the short jacket is named after dancers' costumes. Today, bolero means a Spanish dance in triple time; a short open jacket.
