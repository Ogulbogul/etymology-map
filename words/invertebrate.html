Read the full story

Invertebrate’s story begins in Latin: in meant “without.” Without, combined with vertebratus (having a spine). It reached English by 1826 as invertebrate. Lamarck coined 'animaux sans vertèbres' in 1797. Today, invertebrate means an animal lacking a backbone or spinal column.
