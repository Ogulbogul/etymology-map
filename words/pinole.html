Pinole

3 stages from Nahuatl to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a sweetened, toasted maize flour, traditionally mixed with water as a drink or eaten as a snack

Where does the word "pinole" come from? It comes from Nahuatl pinolli, passing through Spanish before entering English (1800s).
