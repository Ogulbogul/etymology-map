Read the full story

Idli’s story begins in Kannada: iddalige meant “a steamed rice cake.” A savory steamed cake made from fermented rice and lentil batter. It reached English by 1900s as idli. A Kannada text of 920 mentions 'iddalige'; steamed idlis became a South Indian breakfast staple. Today, idli means a savory steamed cake made from fermented rice and lentil batter, popular in South India.
