Coolie

4 stages from Tamil to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means A historical, now offensive, term for an unskilled Asian laborer hired cheaply under colonial-era labor systems.

Where does the word "coolie" come from? It comes from Tamil kuli, passing through Portuguese and Hindi before entering English (17th c.).
