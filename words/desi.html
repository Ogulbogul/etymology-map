Read the full story

Desi’s story begins in Sanskrit: deśa meant “land, country.” Indian music theory contrasted 'deśī', regional styles, with 'mārga', the classical tradition. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Hindi as desī. 'desi ghee' still means ghee made the traditional way, as opposed to factory-made. It reached English by late 1900s as desi. Adopted by the South Asian diaspora in Britain and North America as a name for themselves and their culture. Today, desi means relating to the people, culture or products of South Asia and its diaspora; local, traditional.
