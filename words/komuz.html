Read the full story

Komuz’s story begins in Old Turkic: qobuz meant “stringed instrument.” A general name for stringed instruments among the early Turks. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Kyrgyz as komuz. Accompanied reciters of the Kyrgyz 'Manas' epic. It reached English by 1900s as komuz. Entered English through ethnomusicology and Kyrgyz music. Today, komuz means a fretless three-stringed lute of Kyrgyzstan.
