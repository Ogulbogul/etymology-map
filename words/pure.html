Read the full story

Pure’s story begins in Latin: purus meant “clean, unmixed.” The root of 'purge' and 'purify'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as pur. Used of metals and morals. It reached English by 1200s as pure. 'pure mathematics' dates from the 1500s. Today, pure means not mixed with anything else.
