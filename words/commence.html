Read the full story

Commence’s story begins in Vulgar Latin: cominitiare meant “to begin together.” 'com-' plus 'initiare', to begin. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as comencier. The ordinary Old French verb for begin. It reached English by 1300s as commence. US 'commencement' is a graduation ceremony. Today, commence means to begin.
