Read the full story

Quail’s story begins in Medieval Latin: quaccula meant “quail.” An imitative word from the bird's call, possibly of Germanic origin. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as quaille. Hunted for food in medieval Europe. It reached English by 1300s as quail. Unrelated to 'quail' meaning to cower. Today, quail means a small, short-tailed game bird.
