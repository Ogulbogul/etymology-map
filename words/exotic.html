Read the full story

Exotic’s story begins in Greek: exōtikos meant “foreign.” Greek, from 'exō', outside. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as exotique. Came via Latin 'exoticus'. It reached English by 1590s as exotic. The sense 'strikingly unusual' dates from the 1600s. Today, exotic means originating in a distant foreign country.
