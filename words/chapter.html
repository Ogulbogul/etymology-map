Read the full story

Chapter’s story begins in Latin: capitulum meant “a little head, a small division.” Literally a little head, a diminutive of caput (head), describing a book's small division. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as chapitre. Carried into French for a division of a written work. It reached English by 1200s as chapter. Settled as the English word for a main division of a book. Today, chapter means a main division of a book, or a local branch of an organization.
