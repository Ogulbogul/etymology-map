Read the full story

Poteen’s story begins in Irish: poitín meant “illicit spirit.” 'little pot', for the small pot still; Irish law banned it in 1661, and legal production only returned in 1997. It reached English by 1810s as poteen. The illicit Irish spirit; Bob Quinn's film Poitín (1978) was the first feature film made entirely in Irish. Today, poteen means strong Irish spirit, traditionally distilled illegally from potatoes or grain.
