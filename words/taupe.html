Read the full story

Taupe’s story begins in Latin: talpa meant “a mole.” A mole, the burrowing animal whose fur gave its name to a grey-brown color. By 1800s, the word had taken hold in French as taupe. Carried into French for the color of mole fur. It reached English by 1900s as taupe. French 'mole', from the colour of moleskin. Today, taupe means a brownish-grey color.
