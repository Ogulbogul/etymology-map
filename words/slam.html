Read the full story

Slam’s story begins in Scandinavian: slamra meant “to bang shut.” To bang shut violently, an imitation of the sound itself. It reached English by 1600s as slam. Settled as the English word for shutting something forcefully. Today, slam means to shut or put something down forcefully and loudly.
