Read the full story

Cavalry’s story begins in Latin: caballus meant “a horse.” A horse, especially a workhorse. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Italian as cavalleria. Settled in Italian for soldiers who fought on horseback. It reached English by 1540s as cavalry. Came via French 'cavalerie'; 'cavalier' and 'chivalry' share the root. Today, cavalry means soldiers who fight on horseback, or historically mounted troops.
