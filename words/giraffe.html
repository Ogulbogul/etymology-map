Giraffe

4 stages from Arabic to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a tall African mammal with a very long neck and spotted coat

Where does the word "giraffe" come from? It comes from Arabic zarāfa, passing through Italian and French before entering English (c. 1600s).
