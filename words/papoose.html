Read the full story

Papoose’s story begins in Narragansett: papoos meant “a child, infant.” Meaning "child" or "infant". It reached English by 1600s as papoose. Roger Williams recorded Narragansett 'papoos', child, in 1643. Today, papoose means a Native American infant, or a carrier used to transport a baby.
