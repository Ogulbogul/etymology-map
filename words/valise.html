Read the full story

Valise’s story begins in Medieval Latin: valisia meant “a traveling bag.” A word of uncertain deeper origin for a traveling case. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Italian as valigia. Kept the sense of a traveling bag. From there it passed into French as valise by 1500s. Borrowed from Italian for a leather traveling case. It reached English by 1610s as valise. Adopted for a soldier's or traveler's kit bag. Today, valise means a small suitcase or traveling bag.
