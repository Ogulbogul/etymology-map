Read the full story

Caste’s story begins in Portuguese: casta meant “lineage, breed, race.” Literally lineage or breed, applied by Portuguese traders to India's social divisions. It reached English by 1600s as caste. Settled as the English word for a hereditary social division. Today, caste means a hereditary social class, especially within Hindu society.
