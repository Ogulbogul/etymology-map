Read the full story

Gerbil’s story begins in Arabic: yarbu' / jarbu meant “desert jumping rodent.” The desert rodent's name, literally "flesh of the loins". By 1800s, the word had taken hold in Modern Latin as Gerbillus. Naturalists Latinized the Arabic name for the genus. From there it passed into French as gerbille by 1849. Adapted from the scientific Latin name. It reached English by 1849 as gerbil. Anglicized from French for the small desert rodent. Today, gerbil means a small burrowing desert rodent with long hind legs, often kept as a pet.
