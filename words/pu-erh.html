Pu-erh

2 stages from Chinese to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a fermented, aged variety of Chinese tea named after the Yunnan trading town where it was historically sold

Where does the word "pu-erh" come from? It comes from Chinese Pǔ'ěr, adopted directly into English (late 1900s-2000s).
