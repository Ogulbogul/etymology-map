Read the full story

Mazurka’s story begins in Polish: mazurek meant “a dance from Mazovia.” Literally "a woman of Mazovia", the central Polish region where the dance originated. It reached English by 1800s as mazurka. Adopted as the lively folk dance spread across Europe. Today, mazurka means a lively Polish folk dance in triple time, or the music for it.
