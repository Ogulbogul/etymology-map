Read the full story

Vamoose’s story begins in Latin: vādāmus meant “let us go.” 'let us go', from 'vādere', to go. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Spanish as vamos. 'let's go'. It reached English by 1830s as vamoose. Picked up by Americans in Texas and the Southwest. Today, vamoose means to leave hurriedly.
