Read the full story

Parade’s story begins in Latin: parare meant “to prepare.” The root of 'prepare' and 'repair'. By 1600s, the word had taken hold in French as parade. Came through Spanish 'parada'. It reached English by 1650s as parade. 'hit parade' dates from the 1930s. Today, parade means a public procession.
