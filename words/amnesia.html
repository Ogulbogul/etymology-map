Read the full story

Amnesia’s story begins in Greek: amnesia meant “forgetfulness.” Literally forgetfulness, from a- (without) and mnasthai (to remember). It reached English by 1786 as amnesia. Adopted directly as the medical word for loss of memory. Today, amnesia means a partial or total loss of memory.
