Read the full story

Tabbouleh’s story begins in Arabic: tabil meant “seasoning.” Meant "seasoning" or "spice". By medieval, the word had taken hold in Levantine Arabic as tabbulah. Named the seasoned salad now eaten across the Levant. It reached English by 1939 as tabbouleh. Entered English through Levantine cuisine. Today, tabbouleh means a Levantine salad of bulgur wheat, chopped parsley, tomatoes, mint, and lemon juice.
