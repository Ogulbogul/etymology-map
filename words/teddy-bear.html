Read the full story

Teddy bear’s story begins in Greek: Theódōros meant “gift of God.” 'gift of God', theos 'god' + dōron 'gift', a popular Christian name. By 1902, the word had taken hold in American English as Teddy. A pet form of Theodore; on a Mississippi hunt in November 1902, President Theodore Roosevelt refused to shoot a tethered bear, and Clifford Berryman's cartoon of it went national. It reached English by 1906 as teddy bear. Brooklyn shopkeeper Morris Michtom sold stuffed 'Teddy's bears', while Germany's Steiff company produced its own toy bears at the same time. Today, teddy bear means a soft toy bear.
