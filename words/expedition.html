Read the full story

Expedition’s story begins in Latin: expedire meant “to free the feet, to prepare for a journey.” Literally to free the feet, from ex- (out) and pes (foot), meaning to prepare or set out. It reached English by 1400s as expedition. Settled as the English word for a journey undertaken for a specific purpose. Today, expedition means a journey undertaken by a group for a specific purpose, such as exploration.
