Read the full story

Menemen’s story begins in Turkish: Menemen meant “a town.” A town near İzmir, famous for its vegetables and pottery. Within Turkish, it grew into menemen by 1900s. The dish took the town's name; Turks argue over whether onions belong in it. It reached English by 2000s as menemen. Entered English through Turkish breakfast cafés. Today, menemen means a Turkish dish of eggs scrambled with tomatoes, peppers and onions.
