Chemist

4 stages from Arabic to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a scientist who studies chemistry, or, in British English, a pharmacist

Where does the word "chemist" come from? It comes from Arabic al-kimiya, passing through Medieval Latin and French before entering English (1560s-1790s).
