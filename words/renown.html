Read the full story

Renown’s story begins in Latin: nomen meant “name.” Name, combined with re- (again). By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as renomer. To make famous again, to celebrate by name, from re- and nomer (to name, from nomen). From there it passed into Anglo-French as renoun by medieval. Fame, celebrated reputation, from renomer. It reached English by 1300s as renown. Settled as the English word for widespread fame and reputation. Today, renown means widespread fame and good reputation.
