Read the full story

Summit’s story begins in Latin: summum meant “highest point.” From 'summus', highest. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as somete. A diminutive of 'som', top. It reached English by 1400s as summit. Political 'summits' date from the 1950s. Today, summit means the highest point; a meeting of leaders.
