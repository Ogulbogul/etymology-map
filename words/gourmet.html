Read the full story

Gourmet’s story begins in Old French: groumet meant “a wine merchant's servant.” Originally a wine merchant's assistant or servant. By 1700s, the word had taken hold in French as gourmet. Narrowed to mean a connoisseur of fine food and wine. It reached English by 1800s as gourmet. Adopted directly from French. Today, gourmet means a connoisseur of fine food and drink, or the food itself.
