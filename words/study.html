Read the full story

Study’s story begins in Latin: studium meant “zeal, eagerness.” From 'studere', to be eager. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as estudie. Study as applying oneself eagerly. It reached English by 1300s as study. A room called a 'study' dates from the 1300s. Today, study means time spent learning; a room for work.
