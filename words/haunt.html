Read the full story

Haunt’s story begins in Frankish: haimetan meant “to house, bring home.” A Frankish word related to English 'home'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as hanter. English 'home' shares the Germanic root. It reached English by 1200s as haunt. Ghosts 'haunting' a place dates from the 1500s. Today, haunt means to visit as a ghost; to frequent a place.
