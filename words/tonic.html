Read the full story

Tonic’s story begins in Greek: tonikos meant “of stretching.” Greek, from 'tonos', tension. It reached English by 1640s as tonic. 'tonic water' dates from the 1800s. Today, tonic means a medicine that invigorates; a mixer drink.
