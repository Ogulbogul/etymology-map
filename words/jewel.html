Read the full story

Jewel’s story begins in Latin: jocus meant “jest, game.” The same root as 'joke' and 'juggle'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as jouel. A plaything or trinket, then a precious ornament. It reached English by 1200s as jewel. 'jewel in the crown' was applied to India under British rule. Today, jewel means a precious stone; a treasured person or thing.
