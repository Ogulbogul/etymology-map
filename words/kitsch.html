Read the full story

Kitsch’s story begins in German: kitschen meant “to smear, sketch cheaply.” Possibly meaning "to smear" or "to sketch quickly". Within German, it grew into Kitsch by 1800s. Applied to cheap, sentimental, mass-produced art. It reached English by 1900s as kitsch. Adopted directly from German. Today, kitsch means art, objects, or design considered tacky, garish, or overly sentimental.
