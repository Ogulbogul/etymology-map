Read the full story

Sentence’s story begins in Latin: sententia meant “opinion, judgement.” From 'sentire', to feel. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as sentence. A verdict or opinion. It reached English by 1200s as sentence. The grammatical sense dates from the 1400s. Today, sentence means a set of words forming a statement; a punishment.
