Arabesque

4 stages from Arabic to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means an intricate design of intertwined flowing lines, or a ballet position with one leg extended behind the body

Where does the word "arabesque" come from? It comes from Arabic 'arab, passing through Italian and French before entering English (1786).
