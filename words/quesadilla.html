Read the full story

Quesadilla’s story begins in Spanish: queso meant “cheese.” Cheese, combined with a diminutive suffix for a small cheese-filled dish. By 1600s, the word had taken hold in Mexican Spanish as quesadilla. Settled in Mexico for the folded, cheese-filled tortilla dish. It reached English by 1900s as quesadilla. Adopted directly as Mexican cuisine spread internationally. Today, quesadilla means a Mexican dish of a folded tortilla filled with cheese and other ingredients.
