Read the full story

Stentorian’s story begins in Greek: Stentōr meant “Stentor (a herald).” A herald in Homer's 'Iliad' whose voice was as loud as fifty men's. It reached English by 1600s as stentorian. Formed from his name for any booming voice. Today, stentorian means very loud and powerful (of a voice).
