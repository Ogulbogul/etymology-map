Read the full story

Bouquet’s story begins in Frankish: bosc meant “wood, grove.” A Germanic word meaning "wood" or "grove". By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as bosquet. A diminutive meaning "a small wood or thicket". From there it passed into French as bouquet by 1600s. Came to mean a bunch of flowers gathered like a small thicket. It reached English by 1700s as bouquet. Adopted directly from French. Today, bouquet means an arranged bunch of flowers, or a pleasant aroma, especially of wine.
