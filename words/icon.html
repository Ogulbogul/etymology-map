Read the full story

Icon’s story begins in Greek: eikon meant “an image, likeness.” An image or likeness, especially a religious image. It reached English by 1500s as icon. Settled first for religious images, later any symbolic figure. Today, icon means a person or thing regarded as a representative symbol, or a small screen symbol.
