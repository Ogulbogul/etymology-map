Adobe

4 stages from Egyptian to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a building material made of sun-dried mud and straw, or a structure built from it

Where does the word "adobe" come from? It comes from Egyptian tobe, passing through Arabic and Spanish before entering English (1700s).
