Read the full story

Review’s story begins in Latin: revidere meant “to see again.” 're-' plus 'videre', to see. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as reveue. Past participle of 'reveoir'. It reached English by 1500s as review. Book and film 'reviews' date from the 1600s–1900s. Today, review means a formal assessment; a critical article.
