Read the full story

Torrent’s story begins in Latin: torrens meant “boiling, rushing.” Boiling, roaring, from torrere (to parch, to burn). It reached English by 1600s as torrent. Settled as the English word for a fast-moving, turbulent stream. Today, torrent means a fast-moving, turbulent stream of liquid, or a forceful outpouring.
