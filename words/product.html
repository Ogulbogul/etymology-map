Read the full story

Product’s story begins in Latin: producere meant “to lead forward, to bring forth.” To lead forward, to bring forth, from pro- and ducere, with irregular past participle productus. It reached English by 1400s as product. Settled as the English word for something produced through effort or a process. Today, product means something produced through a natural process, manufacturing, or effort.
