Assassin

4 stages from Arabic to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a person who murders a prominent person, often for political or religious motives

Where does the word "assassin" come from? It comes from Arabic ḥashīshī, passing through Medieval Latin and Italian before entering English (c. 1530s).

ḥashīshī→assassinus→assassino→assassin
