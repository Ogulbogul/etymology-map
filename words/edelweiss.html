Read the full story

Edelweiss’s story begins in German: edili meant “noble.” Old High German word for noble, combined later with the word for white. Within German, it grew into Edelweiß by 1800s. Compound of 'edel' (noble) and 'weiß' (white), naming the alpine flower. It reached English by 1862 as edelweiss. Adopted whole into English for the same Alpine flower. Today, edelweiss means a small white-flowered alpine plant, a symbol of the Alps.
