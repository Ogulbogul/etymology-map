Read the full story

Complete’s story begins in Latin: complere meant “to fill up completely.” To fill up completely, from com- (thoroughly) and plere (to fill), with irregular past participle completus. It reached English by 1300s as complete. Settled as the English word for something having all its parts, finished. Today, complete means having all necessary parts, or finished entirely.
