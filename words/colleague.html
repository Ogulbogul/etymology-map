Read the full story

Colleague’s story begins in Latin: collega meant “partner in office.” 'com-' plus 'legare', to appoint. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as collegue. It reached English by 1520s as colleague. 'college' shares the root. Today, colleague means a person one works with.
