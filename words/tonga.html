Read the full story

Tonga’s story begins in Hindi: tanga meant “a light, two-wheeled horse-drawn carriage.” Named a light two-wheeled horse cart. It reached English by 1870s as tonga. British colonials adopted the word for the common South Asian carriage. Today, tonga means a light, two-wheeled horse-drawn carriage traditionally used for transport in South Asia.
