Kaolin

3 stages from Chinese to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a fine, white clay mineral used in porcelain, paper, and cosmetics

Where does the word "kaolin" come from? It comes from Chinese Gāolǐng, passing through French before entering English (1727).
