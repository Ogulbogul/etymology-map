Read the full story

Affair’s story begins in Latin: ad facere meant “to do.” From the phrase 'à faire', to do. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as afaire. Literally 'something to do'. It reached English by 1300s as affair. 'love affair' dates from the 1700s. Today, affair means an event or matter; a romantic relationship.
