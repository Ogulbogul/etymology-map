Read the full story

Focaccia’s story begins in Latin: focus meant “a hearth.” A hearth, since the flatbread was traditionally baked on the hearth. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Italian as focaccia. Settled in Italian for the flat, oven-baked bread. It reached English by 1900s as focaccia. From Latin 'panis focacius', bread baked on the hearth, 'focus'. Today, focaccia means a flat Italian bread, typically seasoned with olive oil and herbs.
