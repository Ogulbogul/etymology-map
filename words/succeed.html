Read the full story

Succeed’s story begins in Latin: succedere meant “to come after.” To go or come after, from sub- (under, after) and cedere (to go). It reached English by 1400s as succeed. Settled as the English word for achieving a goal, or coming after another in sequence. Today, succeed means to achieve a desired result, or to take over a role after another person.
