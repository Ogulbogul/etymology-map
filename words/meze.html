Read the full story

Meze’s story begins in Persian: maza meant “taste, relish.” Meant "taste" or "relish". By medieval, the word had taken hold in Turkish as meze. Persian 'maza', taste; served with raki or ouzo. It reached English by 1904 as meze. Spread through the cuisines of the former Ottoman lands into English usage. Today, meze means a selection of small Middle Eastern or Mediterranean dishes served together as an appetizer.
