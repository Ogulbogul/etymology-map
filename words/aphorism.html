Read the full story

Aphorism’s story begins in Greek: aphorismos meant “definition.” A definition, from 'horizein', to mark a boundary; the 'Aphorisms' of Hippocrates open with 'Life is short, the art long'. By ancient, the word had taken hold in Late Latin as aphorismus. Used for Hippocrates' medical maxims. From there it passed into French as aphorisme by 1500s. A concise maxim. It reached English by 1520s as aphorism. First for medical rules, then any pithy saying. Today, aphorism means a short, pithy statement of a general truth.
