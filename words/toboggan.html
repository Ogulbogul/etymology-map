Read the full story

Toboggan’s story begins in Mi'kmaq: topaghan meant “a sled.” The name for a sled made of bound wooden strips. It reached English by 1700s-1800s as toboggan. Mi'kmaq 'tophagan', a sled made of bark; toboggan runs became a Montreal pastime. Today, toboggan means a long, flat sled without runners, used for sliding over snow.
