Read the full story

Covenant’s story begins in Latin: convenire meant “to come together, agree.” 'com-', together, plus 'venire', to come. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as covenant. Present participle of 'covenir', to agree. It reached English by 1300s as covenant. Used for the biblical covenant between God and Israel. Today, covenant means a formal agreement.
