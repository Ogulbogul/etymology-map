Read the full story

Stucco’s story begins in Lombardic: stuhhi meant “crust.” A crust or piece, a Germanic word of the Lombards. By 1400s, the word had taken hold in Italian as stucco. Renaissance and Baroque craftsmen made lavish stucco decoration. It reached English by 1590s as stucco. Borrowed for rendered plaster walls and moulding. Today, stucco means fine plaster used on walls and ceilings.
