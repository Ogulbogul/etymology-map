Read the full story

Aegis’s story begins in Greek: aigis meant “the shield of Zeus or Athena.” The shield or protective garment of Zeus and Athena in myth. It reached English by 1600s as aegis. Broadened from the mythical shield to any protective backing or sponsorship. Today, aegis means the protection, backing, or sponsorship of a particular person or organization.
