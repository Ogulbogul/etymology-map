Read the full story

Coleslaw’s story begins in Dutch: koolsla meant “cabbage salad.” Combining "kool" (cabbage) and "sla" (salad). It reached English by 1700s as coleslaw. Adopted through Dutch settlers in colonial America. Today, coleslaw means a salad made of finely shredded raw cabbage, typically with a dressing.
