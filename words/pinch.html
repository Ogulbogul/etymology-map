Read the full story

Pinch’s story begins in Vulgar Latin: pinctiare meant “to pinch.” A Vulgar Latin word of uncertain origin. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as pincier. Norman French 'pinchier'. It reached English by 1200s as pinch. 'pinch' meaning to steal is 1600s slang. Today, pinch means to squeeze between finger and thumb.
