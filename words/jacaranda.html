Read the full story

Jacaranda’s story begins in Tupi-Guarani: yakarandá meant “a flowering tree.” Named the flowering tree with fragrant purple blossoms. By 1600s, the word had taken hold in Portuguese as jacarandá. Its purple-flowering trees line streets in Pretoria, nicknamed 'Jacaranda City'. It reached English by 1750s as jacaranda. Borrowed into English botanical literature. Today, jacaranda means a tropical tree admired for its clusters of vivid purple, trumpet-shaped flowers.
