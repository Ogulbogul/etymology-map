Read the full story

Heritage’s story begins in Late Latin: hereditare meant “to inherit.” From 'heres', heir. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as eritage. Property passed down. It reached English by 1200s as heritage. 'cultural heritage' dates from the 1900s. Today, heritage means valued traditions passed down.
