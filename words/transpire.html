Read the full story

Transpire’s story begins in Latin: spirare meant “to breathe.” To breathe, combined with trans- (across). By ancient, the word had taken hold in Latin as transpirare. To breathe across, to emit through pores, from trans- and spirare. It reached English by 1600s as transpire. Settled as the English word for becoming known, or emitting vapor through pores. Today, transpire means to become known or happen, or for a plant to release water vapor through its leaves.
