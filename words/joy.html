Read the full story

Joy’s story begins in Latin: gaudium meant “joy, delight.” From 'gaudere', to rejoice. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as joie. The same root as 'enjoy' and 'jewel'. It reached English by 1200s as joy. 'joyride' dates from the 1900s. Today, joy means a feeling of great pleasure.
