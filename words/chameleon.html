Read the full story

Chameleon’s story begins in Greek: khamaileon meant “ground lion.” Literally ground lion, from khamai (on the ground) and leon (lion). It reached English by 1300s as chameleon. Settled as the word for the color-changing lizard. Today, chameleon means a lizard that changes color, or a person who adapts easily.
