Cross

4 stages from Latin to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a shape formed by two intersecting lines, especially as a religious symbol

Where does the word "cross" come from? It comes from Latin crux, passing through Old Irish and Old Norse before entering English (1100s).
