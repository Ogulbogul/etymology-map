Read the full story

Nice’s story begins in Latin: nescius meant “ignorant.” 'ne-', not, plus 'scire', to know. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as nice. Meant foolish or naive. It reached English by 1300s as nice. Shifted through 'fussy' and 'precise' to 'pleasant' by the 1700s. Today, nice means pleasant; kind.
