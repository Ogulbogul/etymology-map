Read the full story

Sovereign’s story begins in Vulgar Latin: superanus meant “above, superior.” Above others, from super (above). By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as soverain. Carried into French for one holding supreme power. It reached English by 1300s as sovereign. Settled as the English word for a supreme ruler or supreme authority. Today, sovereign means a supreme ruler, or possessing supreme power and independent authority.
