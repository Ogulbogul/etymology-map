Read the full story

Caress’s story begins in Latin: carus meant “dear.” The root of 'charity' and 'cherish'. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in Italian as carezza. An endearment; came into English via French 'caresse'. It reached English by 1640s as caress. Used as a verb from the 1650s. Today, caress means a gentle loving touch.
