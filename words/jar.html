Read the full story

Jar’s story begins in Arabic: jarra meant “an earthenware vessel.” The Arabic name for an earthenware vessel. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Spanish as jarra. Arabic 'jarra', earthen water vessel. It reached English by 1500s-1600s as jar. Adopted via French and Spanish trade in pottery. Today, jar means a cylindrical container, usually made of glass or ceramic.
