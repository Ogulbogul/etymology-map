Read the full story

Pierce’s story begins in Vulgar Latin: pertusiare meant “to bore through.” From 'pertundere', to punch through. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as percier. Spelled 'perce' in early English. It reached English by 1200s as pierce. 'piercing' for jewellery dates from the 1900s. Today, pierce means to go into or through with a sharp point.
