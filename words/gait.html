Read the full story

Gait’s story begins in Old Norse: gata meant “a way, a path.” A way, a path, later extended to describe a manner of walking. It reached English by 1400s as gait. Settled as the English word for a person's manner of walking. Today, gait means a person's particular manner or style of walking.
