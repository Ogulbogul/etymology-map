Read the full story

Umiak’s story begins in Inuktitut: umiaq meant “a large open boat covered in animal skin.” Named the large open skin boat paddled by women for transport and whaling. It reached English by 1750s as umiak. Borrowed by explorers and whalers describing Inuit watercraft. Today, umiak means a large, open Arctic boat traditionally covered in animal skins and used for transport or whaling.
