Read the full story

Strudel’s story begins in German: Strudel meant “a whirlpool.” Meaning "whirlpool" or "eddy", describing the pastry's rolled layers. It reached English by 1800s as strudel. German 'whirlpool', from its rolled layers; Vienna's apple strudel is the classic. Today, strudel means a rolled pastry made of thin dough with a sweet filling, such as apple.
