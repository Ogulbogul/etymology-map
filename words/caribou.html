Read the full story

Caribou’s story begins in Mi'kmaq: qalipu meant “a snow-digging animal.” Possibly meaning 'snow shoveler', describing how the animal digs through snow. By 1600s, the word had taken hold in Canadian French as caribou. Mi'kmaq 'qalipu', snow-shoveller, for how the animal paws through snow to feed. It reached English by 1660s as caribou. Used in North America, while the same animal in Eurasia is called reindeer. Today, caribou means a North American reindeer, found in Arctic and subarctic regions.
