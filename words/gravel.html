Read the full story

Gravel’s story begins in Gaulish: grava meant “gravel, sand.” A Gaulish word; the root also of 'grave' as a shore. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as gravele. A diminutive of 'grave', sandy shore. It reached English by 1200s as gravel. 'gravelly' voice dates from the 1800s. Today, gravel means small loose stones.
