Read the full story

Chinchilla’s story begins in Quechua/Aymara: chinchilla meant “the chinchilla rodent.” The name for the small, soft-furred rodent of the Andes. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in Spanish as chinchilla. Spanish colonists traded chinchilla fur so heavily that the wild animal nearly went extinct by 1900. It reached English by 1600s as chinchilla. Settled as the English word for the small rodent prized for its soft fur. Today, chinchilla means a small South American rodent with soft, dense fur, kept as a pet or for fur.
