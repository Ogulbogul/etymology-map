Read the full story

Treaty’s story begins in Latin: tractare meant “to handle, to negotiate.” To handle, to manage, to negotiate. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as traite. Carried into French for a formal negotiated agreement. It reached English by 1300s as treaty. Settled as the English word for a formal agreement between nations. Today, treaty means a formally negotiated agreement between two or more countries.
