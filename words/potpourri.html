Read the full story

Potpourri’s story begins in French: pot pourri meant “a mixed stew.” Literally "rotten pot", originally a stew of many mixed ingredients. Within French, it grew into potpourri by 1700s. Extended to a jar of dried, mixed flower petals. It reached English by 1700s as potpourri. Adopted directly from French. Today, potpourri means a mixture of dried petals and spices used for scent, or any varied collection.
