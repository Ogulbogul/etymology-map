Read the full story

Coracle’s story begins in Welsh: corwgl meant “round boat.” A round boat of wicker covered with hide (later tarred cloth); coracle-men still fish the River Teifi at Cenarth. It reached English by 1540s as coracle. An ancient craft: Julius Caesar's Civil War describes him copying British-style boats of wicker and hide in Spain in 49 BC. Today, coracle means a small round boat of wickerwork covered with watertight material.
