Read the full story

Flask’s story begins in Medieval Latin: flasco meant “bottle.” A Medieval Latin word, possibly Germanic. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as flasque. Used for soldiers' powder flasks; Italian 'fiasco' comes from the same word. It reached English by 1500s as flask. Later any narrow-necked bottle, including a pocket flask for spirits. Today, flask means a narrow-necked bottle.
