Spitz’s story begins in German: spitz meant “pointed.” Meaning "pointed", describing the dog's sharp muzzle and ears. It reached English by 1800s as spitz. German 'pointed', for the sharp muzzle; Pomeranians are spitz dogs. Today, spitz means any of a group of dog breeds with pointed ears, muzzle, and a thick coat.
