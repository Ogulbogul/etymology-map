Read the full story

Carnivore’s story begins in Latin: caro meant “flesh, meat.” Flesh, meat, combined with vorare (to devour). It reached English by 1830s as carnivore. Adopted by biologists for an animal that primarily eats meat. Today, carnivore means an animal that feeds primarily on the flesh of other animals.
