Read the full story

Chauvinism’s story begins in French: Nicolas Chauvin meant “a soldier's name.” A soldier famed, likely apocryphally, for his excessive devotion to Napoleon and France. It reached English by 1870s as chauvinism. Coined from the name for excessive, unreasoning patriotism or group loyalty. Today, chauvinism means excessive or prejudiced devotion to one's own group, cause, or gender.
