Read the full story

Banquet’s story begins in Frankish: bank meant “bench.” A Germanic word, the same as English 'bench'. By 1400s, the word had taken hold in Italian as banchetto. A small bench, then the feast eaten sitting on it. It reached English by 1480s as banquet. Arrived via French 'banquet'. Today, banquet means an elaborate formal meal.
