Read the full story

Rover’s story begins in Middle Dutch: rover meant “robber, pirate.” From 'roven', to rob, related to 'bereave'. By 1300s, the word had taken hold in Middle English as rover. First meant a sea-robber. It reached English by 1970s as rover. Blended with 'rove', to wander; now used for robotic vehicles exploring Mars. Today, rover means a person who wanders; a vehicle for exploring terrain, especially on other planets.
