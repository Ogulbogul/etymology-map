Read the full story

Chachalaca’s story begins in Nahuatl: chachalacani meant “a chatterer, gossiper.” An onomatopoeic word meaning 'to chatter', describing the bird's loud repeated call. By 1600s, the word had taken hold in Spanish as chachalaca. An imitation of the bird's loud call. It reached English by 1810s as chachalaca. Borrowed into English ornithological terminology. Today, chachalaca means a loud, chicken-sized tropical bird known for its raucous, repetitive call.
