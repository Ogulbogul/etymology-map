Read the full story

Wat’s story begins in Amharic: wat meant “a spicy stew.” A thick, spicy stew central to Ethiopian and Eritrean cuisine. It reached English by 1900s as wat. Amharic 'wot', a stew eaten on injera; doro wat, chicken stew, is Ethiopia's national dish. Today, wat means a thick, spicy stew central to Ethiopian and Eritrean cuisine, often served with injera.
