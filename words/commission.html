Read the full story

Commission’s story begins in Latin: commissio meant “a bringing together.” From 'committere', to entrust. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as commission. An authority entrusted. It reached English by 1300s as commission. Sales 'commissions' date from the 1700s. Today, commission means an instruction; a payment to an agent; a body of officials.
