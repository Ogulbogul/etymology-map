Read the full story

Lemur’s story begins in Latin: lemures meant “spirits of the dead.” The restless spirits of the dead in Roman belief. By 1758, the word had taken hold in New Latin as lemur. Coined by Linnaeus for the wide-eyed nocturnal primate, evoking ghostly spirits. It reached English by 1795 as lemur. Adopted directly for the nocturnal primate native to Madagascar. Today, lemur means a wide-eyed, nocturnal primate native to Madagascar.
