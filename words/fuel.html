Read the full story

Fuel’s story begins in Latin: focus meant “hearth.” The root of 'focus', originally a fireplace. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as fouaille. A right to take firewood. It reached English by 1300s as fuel. 'fuel' for engines dates from the 1800s. Today, fuel means material burned to produce heat or power.
