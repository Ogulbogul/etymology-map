Coca

3 stages from Quechua to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means a South American shrub whose leaves are chewed as a mild stimulant or processed into cocaine

Where does the word "coca" come from? It comes from Quechua kuka, passing through Spanish before entering English (1700s-1800s).
