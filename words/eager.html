Read the full story

Eager’s story begins in Latin: acer meant “sharp, keen.” The same root as 'acid' and 'acrid'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as aigre. Meant sour or harsh; it also gives 'vinegar', sour wine. It reached English by 1300s as eager. The sense of keenness won out over sourness. Today, eager means strongly wanting to do or have something.
