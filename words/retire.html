Read the full story

Retire’s story begins in Old French: tirer meant “to draw.” An Old French verb of uncertain origin. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as retirer. To pull back. It reached English by 1530s as retire. The sense 'stop working for good' dates from the 1600s. Today, retire means to leave one's job and stop working.
