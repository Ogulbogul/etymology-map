Read the full story

Nuisance’s story begins in Latin: nocere meant “to harm.” The root of 'noxious' and 'innocent'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as nuisance. A legal term for harm. It reached English by 1400s as nuisance. The sense 'annoying thing' dates from the 1600s. Today, nuisance means an annoying person or thing.
