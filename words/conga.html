Read the full story

Conga’s story begins in Spanish: Congo meant “the Congo region.” Named after the Congo region of Central Africa, source of the drum and dance tradition. By 1800s-1900s, the word had taken hold in Cuban Spanish as conga. Developed into a line dance and drum tradition in Cuba. It reached English by 1930s-1940s as conga. Adopted as the Cuban dance craze spread internationally. Today, conga means a Latin American line dance, or a tall, narrow drum played with the hands.
