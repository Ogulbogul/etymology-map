Read the full story

Reason’s story begins in Latin: ratio meant “reckoning, reason.” From 'reri', to reckon. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as raison. The Latin t softened in French. It reached English by 1200s as reason. The Age of Reason referred to the 1700s. Today, reason means a cause or explanation; the power to think logically.
