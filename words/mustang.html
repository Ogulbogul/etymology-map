Read the full story

Mustang’s story begins in Spanish: mesta meant “stockmen's guild.” The powerful guild of Castilian sheep and cattle owners, which rounded up stray animals. By 1600s, the word had taken hold in Mexican Spanish as mesteño. A stray, ownerless animal, belonging to the 'mesta'. It reached English by 1800s as mustang. First recorded in 1808; descended from horses brought by the Spanish. Today, mustang means a small wild or half-wild horse of the American West.
