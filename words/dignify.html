Read the full story

Dignify’s story begins in Late Latin: dignificare meant “to make worthy.” 'dignus', worthy, plus 'facere', to make. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as dignifier. Medieval Latin 'dignificare', to make worthy, joined 'dignus' (worthy) and 'facere' (to make). It reached English by 1400s as dignify. 'dignified' followed. Today, dignify means to make something seem worthy.
