Read the full story

Teff’s story begins in Amharic: teffa meant “a minute, easily-scattered cereal grain.” Named for its tiny seeds, easily 'lost' during harvesting and threshing. It reached English by 1800s as teff. The world's smallest grain, used to make injera. Today, teff means a tiny, nutrient-dense cereal grain native to Ethiopia, ground into flour for injera bread and prized today as a gluten-free grain.
