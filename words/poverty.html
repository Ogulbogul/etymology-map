Read the full story

Poverty’s story begins in Latin: paupertas meant “poverty.” From 'pauper', poor. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as poverté. The root of 'poor' and 'impoverish'. It reached English by 1100s as poverty. One of the monastic vows. Today, poverty means the state of being extremely poor.
