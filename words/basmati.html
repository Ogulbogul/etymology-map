Read the full story

Basmati’s story begins in Sanskrit: vasmati meant “fragrant.” Literally "fragrant", describing the aromatic rice. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Hindi/Punjabi as basmati. The name for the long-grain aromatic rice variety. It reached English by 1900s as basmati. Adopted as the rice variety became popular internationally. Today, basmati means a variety of long-grain, aromatic rice from South Asia.
