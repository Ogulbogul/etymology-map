Read the full story

Carol’s story begins in Late Latin: choraula meant “choral song.” From Greek 'khoraulēs', a flute-player accompanying a chorus. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as carole. A circle dance with singing. It reached English by 1300s as carol. Narrowed to Christmas songs in the 1500s. Today, carol means a religious song sung at Christmas.
