Read the full story

Juche’s story begins in Korean: juche meant “self-reliance; the guiding political philosophy of North Korea.” Meaning 'self-reliance' or 'subject', developed as North Korea's official state ideology. It reached English by 1970s-1980s as juche. Korean 'self-reliance', promoted by Kim Il-sung from 1955. Today, juche means north Korea's official state ideology emphasizing national self-reliance.
