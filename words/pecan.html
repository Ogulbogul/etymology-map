Read the full story

Pecan’s story begins in Illinois (Algonquian): pakani meant “a hard-shelled nut.” A general term for nuts requiring a stone to crack. It reached English by 1700s as pecan. Illinois 'pakani', a nut too hard to crack by hand; Thomas Jefferson planted pecan trees at Monticello. Today, pecan means a smooth, edible nut from a North American hickory tree.
