Read the full story

Bullet’s story begins in Latin: bulla meant “bubble, knob.” The root of 'bubble' and 'bullion'. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as boulette. A diminutive of 'boule', ball. It reached English by 1550s as bullet. 'bullet points' date from the 1980s. Today, bullet means a projectile fired from a gun.
