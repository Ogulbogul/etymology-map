Read the full story

Lottery’s story begins in Italian/Germanic: lotto meant “a lot, share.” Meaning "a lot" or "portion", of Germanic origin. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in Italian as lotteria. Named a game of chance for distributing prizes by lot. It reached English by 1500s-1600s as lottery. Queen Elizabeth I held England's first state lottery in 1569. Today, lottery means a game of chance in which winners are selected by random drawing.
