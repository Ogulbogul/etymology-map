Read the full story

Kilim’s story begins in Persian: gilim meant “a coarse-woven blanket.” A coarse-woven blanket or rug. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Ottoman Turkish as kilim. A flat, tapestry-woven rug or carpet, traditional across Central Asia and the Middle East. It reached English by 1800s as kilim. Adopted directly for the distinctive flat-woven textile. Today, kilim means a flat, tapestry-woven rug or carpet, traditional to Central Asia and the Middle East.
