Read the full story

Ghat’s story begins in Sanskrit: ghattah meant “a landing place, riverbank.” Meant 'landing place'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Hindi as ghat. Came to mean steps down to a river, or a mountain pass. It reached English by 1783 as ghat. British travelers in India adopted the word for both riverside steps and mountain passes. Today, ghat means a set of steps leading down to a river (especially for bathing or cremation), or a mountain pass in South Asia.
