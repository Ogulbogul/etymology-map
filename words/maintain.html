Read the full story

Maintain’s story begins in Latin: manu tenere meant “to hold by hand.” Literally to hold by hand, from manus (hand) and tenere (to hold). By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as maintenir. Carried into French for keeping something in a steady state. It reached English by 1300s as maintain. Settled as the English word for keeping something in good condition. Today, maintain means to keep something in good condition, or to continue a state or activity.
