Read the full story

Bunraku’s story begins in Japanese: Bunraku-za meant “a puppet theater troupe's name.” Named after Uemura Bunrakuken, a puppeteer whose troupe revived the traditional art. It reached English by 20th century as bunraku. Borrowed as a general term for Japanese traditional puppet theater. Today, bunraku means a traditional Japanese form of puppet theater performed with large, intricately controlled puppets.
