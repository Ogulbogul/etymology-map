Read the full story

Peri’s story begins in Avestan: pairika meant “evil supernatural being.” Named a malevolent supernatural being in ancient Iranian belief. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Persian as pari. Softened over time into a beautiful, winged nymph-like spirit. It reached English by 1777 as peri. Entered English literature describing graceful supernatural beings of Persian myth. Today, peri means a beautiful, graceful supernatural being in Persian mythology, often depicted with wings.
