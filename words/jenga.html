Read the full story

Jenga’s story begins in Swahili: kujenga meant “to build or construct.” The verb inspired the name of a stacking-block game created by Leslie Scott, who grew up speaking Swahili in East Africa. It reached English by 1983 as Jenga. Trademarked as the name of the wooden block-stacking game. Today, jenga means a game in which players remove and restack wooden blocks from a tower without making it fall; loosely, any precarious stack.
