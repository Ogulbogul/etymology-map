Read the full story

Leviathan’s story begins in Hebrew: livyatan meant “a great sea monster.” A huge sea monster described in biblical texts. By ancient, the word had taken hold in Late Latin as leviathan. Adopted through the Latin translation of scripture. It reached English by medieval as leviathan. Adopted through Bible translation, later used for anything huge and powerful. Today, leviathan means something of enormous size or power, especially a sea creature or institution.
