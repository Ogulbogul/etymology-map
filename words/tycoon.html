Read the full story

Tycoon’s story begins in Chinese: dà jūn meant “great prince.” An old written compound meaning "great prince" or "great army". By 1850s-1860s, the word had taken hold in Japanese as taikun. Used as a title for the shogun when dealing with foreign powers. It reached English by 1860s as tycoon. Brought back to the US by officials who dealt with Japan; applied to Lincoln, then business magnates. Today, tycoon means a wealthy, powerful business leader.
