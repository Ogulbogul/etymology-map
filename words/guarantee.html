Read the full story

Guarantee’s story begins in Frankish: warjan meant “to protect, to guard.” To protect, to guard, a Germanic root of the Franks. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as garantie. Adapted from Frankish for a formal assurance or promise. It reached English by 1600s as guarantee. Settled as the English word for a formal promise that something will happen. Today, guarantee means a formal promise or assurance that something will happen or meet a certain standard.
