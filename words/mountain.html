Read the full story

Mountain’s story begins in Latin: mons meant “a mountain.” A mountain, a large mass of rock and earth. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as montaigne. Carried into French for a large natural elevation of land. It reached English by 1200s as mountain. Settled as the English word for a large natural elevation of land. Today, mountain means a large natural elevation of land, higher and steeper than a hill.
