Read the full story

Genetic’s story begins in Greek: génesis meant “origin.” 'origin, birth'; the Greek Old Testament gave the name Genesis to the first book of the Bible. It reached English by 1905 as genetic, genetics. 'genetic' (1830s) first meant 'relating to origins'; William Bateson proposed 'genetics' for the study of heredity in a letter of April 1905 to Adam Sedgwick, reviving Gregor Mendel's forgotten pea experiments. Today, genetic means relating to genes or heredity.
