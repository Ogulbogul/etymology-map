Read the full story

Fruit’s story begins in Latin: fructus meant “enjoyment, produce.” From 'frui', to enjoy. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as fruit. Any produce of the earth. It reached English by 1100s as fruit. 'fruitful' and 'fruition' followed. Today, fruit means the sweet, seed-bearing part of a plant.
