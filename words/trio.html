Read the full story

Trio’s story begins in Latin: tre meant “three.” Three, the root of any group of three. By 1700s, the word had taken hold in Italian as trio. Adopted into Italian musical vocabulary for a group of three performers. It reached English by 1720s as trio. Settled as the English word for any group of three. Today, trio means a group of three people, especially musicians, or a piece for three performers.
