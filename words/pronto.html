Read the full story

Pronto’s story begins in Latin: promptus meant “ready.” Ready, at hand. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Spanish as pronto. Quick, soon. It reached English by 1850s as pronto. Spread from the Southwest into general slang. Today, pronto means promptly; quickly.
