3 stages from Hindi to English.

Stage of in this word's full journey.

Today it means The largest antelope of Asia, native to India, with males having a distinctive blue-grey coat.

Where does the word "nilgai" come from? It comes from Hindi nil, passing through Hindi before entering English (1882).
