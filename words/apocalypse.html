Read the full story

Apocalypse’s story begins in Greek: apokalypsis meant “uncovering.” An uncovering or revelation, from 'apo', off, and 'kalyptein', to cover. By ancient, the word had taken hold in Late Latin as apocalypsis. The title of the Book of Revelation in the Latin Bible. It reached English by 1100s as apocalypse. Its sense of a world-ending catastrophe comes from the visions in the Book of Revelation. Today, apocalypse means the complete destruction of the world; a revelation.
