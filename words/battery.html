Read the full story

Battery’s story begins in Vulgar Latin: battuere meant “to beat.” The root of 'battle' and 'batter'. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as batterie. A group of cannons firing together. It reached English by 1530s as battery. Benjamin Franklin applied it to linked electrical jars in 1749. Today, battery means a device storing electricity; a group of guns.
