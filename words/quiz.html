Read the full story

Quiz’s story begins in English: quiz meant “eccentric person; a hoax.” Origin unknown, first meaning an odd or eccentric person; the story that Dublin theatre owner Richard Daly invented it for a bet in 1791 is a later legend. It reached English by 1867 as quiz. Perhaps influenced by 'inquisitive'; US students used it from 1867 for an oral test, and radio quiz shows spread from the late 1930s. Today, quiz means a set of questions as a test or game.
