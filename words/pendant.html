Read the full story

Pendant’s story begins in Latin: pendere meant “to hang.” The root of 'pendulum'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as pendant. Present participle of 'pendre'. It reached English by 1300s as pendant. Jewellery 'pendants' followed. Today, pendant means a piece of jewellery hanging from a chain.
