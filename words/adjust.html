Read the full story

Adjust’s story begins in Latin: juxta meant “near, beside.” The source of Old French 'ajoster', to join, later confused with 'just'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as ajoster. To join together. It reached English by 1610s as adjust. The spelling was remodelled on 'just', fair. Today, adjust means to alter slightly to fit.
