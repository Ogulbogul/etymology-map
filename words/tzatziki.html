Read the full story

Tzatziki’s story begins in Ottoman Turkish: cacik meant “a yogurt and cucumber dish.” A cold yogurt and cucumber dish, common across the eastern Mediterranean. By 1900s, the word had taken hold in Greek as tzatziki. From Turkish 'cacık'. It reached English by 1950s as tzatziki. Today, tzatziki means a Greek condiment made from yogurt, cucumber, and garlic.
