Read the full story

Energy’s story begins in Greek: energeia meant “activity, operation.” Literally "activity" or "operation", from "ergon" (work). By ancient, the word had taken hold in Late Latin as energia. Adopted through Greek philosophical writing. From there it passed into French as energie by medieval. Carried the concept into French. It reached English by medieval as energy. Later adopted as the physics term for the capacity to do work. Today, energy means the capacity to do work or produce change, or physical vigor.
