Read the full story

Coolabah’s story begins in Yuwaalaraay: gulabaa meant “coolabah tree.” A eucalyptus of inland floodplains; the Burke and Wills 'Dig Tree' at Cooper Creek, blazed in 1861, is a coolabah. It reached English by 1860s as coolabah. Banjo Paterson's 'Waltzing Matilda' (1895) sets its swagman 'under the shade of a coolibah tree'. Today, coolabah means a eucalyptus tree of inland Australian watercourses.
