Read the full story

Blitz’s story begins in German: Blitz meant “lightning.” Within German, it grew into Blitzkrieg by 1930s. Combining "Blitz" with "Krieg" (war), a rapid military offensive. It reached English by 1940 as blitz. Shortened during the German bombing campaign against Britain. Today, blitz means a sudden, intense military attack, or any concentrated burst of effort.
