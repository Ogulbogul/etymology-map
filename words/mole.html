Read the full story

Mole’s story begins in Nahuatl: mōlli meant “sauce, something ground.” Meant simply 'sauce' or 'something ground', the base word for many Aztec sauces. By 1600s, the word had taken hold in Spanish as mole. Adopted into Mexican Spanish for elaborate chili-based sauces. It reached English by 1930s as mole. Borrowed into English through Mexican cuisine. Today, mole means a rich, complex Mexican sauce, often containing chili peppers, spices, and sometimes chocolate.
