Read the full story

Butler’s story begins in Medieval Latin: buticularius meant “cup-bearer.” From 'buticula', bottle. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as bouteillier. The servant in charge of the wine. It reached English by 1200s as butler. Later the head servant of a household. Today, butler means the chief manservant of a house.
