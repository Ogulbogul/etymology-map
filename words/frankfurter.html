Read the full story

Frankfurter’s story begins in German: Frankfurt meant “the city of Frankfurt.” The German city associated with a style of smoked sausage. Within German, it grew into Frankfurter by 1800s. Named the smoked sausage in the Frankfurt style. It reached English by 1800s-1900s as frankfurter. Brought to the US by German immigrants. Today, frankfurter means a smoked sausage, typically served in a long bread roll as a hot dog.
