Read the full story

Jerboa’s story begins in Arabic: jarbu meant “jumping desert rodent.” Literally "flesh of the loins," naming the rodent for its powerful hind legs. By 1600s, the word had taken hold in Modern Latin as jerboa. A jumping desert rodent of North Africa and Asia. It reached English by 1660s as jerboa. Entered English as the common name for the small jumping rodent. Today, jerboa means a small jumping desert rodent with long hind legs, native to North Africa and Asia.
