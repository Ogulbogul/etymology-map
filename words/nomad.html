Read the full story

Nomad’s story begins in Greek: nomas meant “wandering herder.” 'roaming to find pasture', from 'nemein', to graze; the Romans' 'Numidia' in North Africa was named from it. By ancient, the word had taken hold in Latin as nomas. A wandering herdsman. From there it passed into French as nomade by 1500s. Nomadic peoples. It reached English by 1580s as nomad. 'digital nomad' dates from the 1990s. Today, nomad means a member of a people with no permanent home who travel from place to place.
