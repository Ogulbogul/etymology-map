Read the full story

Obstetric’s story begins in Latin: obstetrix meant “a midwife.” A midwife, from obstare (to stand before, be present). It reached English by 1740s as obstetric. Settled as the medical word for the care of childbirth. Today, obstetric means relating to pregnancy, childbirth, and the period following.
