Read the full story

Lassi’s story begins in Sanskrit: lasya meant “stickiness; juice.” Root meaning 'stickiness' (an alternate theory traces it to rasa, 'juice'). By medieval, the word had taken hold in Punjabi as lassi. Became the name of the churned yogurt drink. It reached English by 1882 as lassi. British writers on India adopted the word. Today, lassi means a chilled, blended yogurt drink from South Asia, served sweet, salty, or flavored with fruit.
