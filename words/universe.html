Read the full story

Universe’s story begins in Latin: universus meant “turned into one, combined into a whole.” Turned into one, combined into a whole, from unus (one) and vertere (to turn). It reached English by 1580s as universe. Settled as the English word for all of space and everything in it. Today, universe means all of space and everything that exists within it.
