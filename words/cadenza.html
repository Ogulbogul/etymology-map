Read the full story

Cadenza’s story begins in Italian: cadenza meant “a falling, a cadence.” Literally a cadence, an ornamental solo passage near a piece's close. It reached English by 1770s as cadenza. Adopted directly for a soloist's improvised showcase passage. Today, cadenza means a virtuosic solo passage, often improvised, near the end of a musical movement.
