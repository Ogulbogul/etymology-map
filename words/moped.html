Read the full story

Moped’s story begins in Swedish: (trampcykel med) mo(tor och) ped(aler) meant “a pedal cycle fitted with an engine.” Coined by Swedish journalist Harald Nielsen as a blend meaning 'pedal cycle with motor and pedals'. By 1950s, the word had taken hold in German as Moped. The Swedish coinage passed into German usage as manufacturing and trade spread the term. It reached English by 1956 onward as moped. Borrowed into English for the same lightweight motorbike. Today, moped means a small motorcycle, originally pedal-assisted, with a low-powered engine.
