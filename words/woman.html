Read the full story

Woman’s story begins in Old English: wīfmann meant “female person.” A compound of 'wīf', woman, and 'mann', person. By 1100s-1400s, the word had taken hold in Middle English as wimman. The 'f' disappeared and the vowel shifted, giving 'wimman' and later 'womman'. It reached English by 1500s as woman. The plural 'women' still keeps the old 'wimmen' pronunciation. Today, woman means an adult female human.
