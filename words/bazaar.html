Read the full story

Bazaar’s story begins in Persian: bazar meant “marketplace.” A marketplace where goods are bought and sold. By ottoman, the word had taken hold in Turkish as bazar. Carried into Turkish trade vocabulary for the marketplace. It reached English by 1600s as bazaar. Settled as the English word for a marketplace or charity sale. Today, bazaar means a marketplace, especially in the Middle East, or a sale of goods for charity.
