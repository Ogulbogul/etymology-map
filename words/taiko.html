Read the full story

Taiko’s story begins in Japanese: taiko meant “a drum, especially a large barrel-shaped one.” The general word for drum, later specialized for large ensemble performance drums. It reached English by 1950s-1960s as taiko. Borrowed as Japanese-American drumming ensembles popularized the art form in the US. Today, taiko means large Japanese drums, or the ensemble drumming performance style built around them.
