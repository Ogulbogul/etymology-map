Geta’s story begins in Japanese: geta meant “elevated wooden sandals.” Named traditional wooden sandals raised on two supporting teeth. It reached English by 1800s-1900s as geta. Raised on wooden teeth to keep the feet above mud and snow. Today, geta means traditional Japanese wooden sandals raised on two supporting blocks.
