Read the full story

Publicize’s story begins in Latin: populus meant “the people.” The people, giving rise to publicus (of the people). Within Latin, it grew into publicus by ancient. Of the people, belonging to the public, from populus. From there it passed into English as public by 1300s. Settled as the English word for something open to or shared by everyone. It reached English by 1911 as publicize. Formed from public with the English suffix -ize, for making something widely known. Today, publicize means to make something widely known to the public.
