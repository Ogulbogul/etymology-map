Read the full story

Excuse’s story begins in Latin: excusare meant “to free from blame.” 'ex-', out of, plus 'causa', accusation. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as escuser. The root of 'cause' is also in 'accuse'. It reached English by 1200s as excuse. 'excuse me' as a polite phrase is from the 1500s. Today, excuse means to forgive a fault.
