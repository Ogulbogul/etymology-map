Read the full story

Diagram’s story begins in Greek: diagramma meant “figure drawn by lines.” Greek, 'dia-', through, plus 'graphein', to write. It reached English by 1610s as diagram. 'Venn diagram' dates from 1880. Today, diagram means a simplified drawing.
