Read the full story

Scissors’s story begins in Late Latin: cisoria meant “cutting tools.” From 'caedere', to cut, confused with 'scindere', to split. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as cisoires. The 'sc' spelling came from confusion with Latin 'scissor', a cutter. It reached English by 1300s as scissors. 'scissor kick' dates from the 1900s. Today, scissors means a cutting tool with two crossing blades.
