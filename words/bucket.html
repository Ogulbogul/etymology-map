Read the full story

Bucket’s story begins in Old English: buc meant “belly, trunk.” An Old English word that blended with Anglo-Norman 'buquet'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as buket. Used for a washtub or pail. It reached English by 1200s as bucket. 'kick the bucket' dates from the 1700s. Today, bucket means an open container with a handle for carrying liquid.
