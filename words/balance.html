Read the full story

Balance’s story begins in Vulgar Latin: bilanx meant “having two pans.” 'bi-', two, plus 'lanx', dish or scale pan. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as balance. A pair of scales. It reached English by 1200s as balance. Bank 'balance' dates from the 1500s. Today, balance means an even distribution of weight; a remaining sum.
