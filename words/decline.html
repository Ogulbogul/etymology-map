Read the full story

Decline’s story begins in Latin: declinare meant “to bend aside.” 'de-' plus 'clinare', to lean. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as decliner. Also grammatical declension. It reached English by 1300s as decline. The sense 'politely refuse' dates from the 1600s. Today, decline means to become smaller; to refuse politely.
