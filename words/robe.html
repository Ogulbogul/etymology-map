Read the full story

Robe’s story begins in Frankish: rauba meant “booty, spoils.” A Germanic word related to 'rob'; clothing was the typical plunder. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as robe. 'wardrobe' means 'guard for robes'. It reached English by 1200s as robe. 'bathrobe' dates from the 1900s. Today, robe means a long loose outer garment.
