Read the full story

Traffic’s story begins in Italian: traffico meant “trade.” An Italian merchants' word of uncertain origin. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in French as trafique. Commerce and trade goods. It reached English by 1500s as traffic. Road 'traffic' dates from the 1800s. Today, traffic means vehicles moving on roads; illegal trade.
