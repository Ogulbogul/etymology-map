Read the full story

Sriracha’s story begins in Thai: Si Racha meant “a Thai coastal town name.” A coastal town in Thailand where the spicy chili sauce was first made. It reached English by 1980s as sriracha. Adopted directly for the spicy, garlicky chili sauce named after the town. Today, sriracha means a spicy, garlicky chili sauce named after its town of origin in Thailand.
