Read the full story

Reservoir’s story begins in Latin: reservare meant “to keep back, preserve.” Meaning "to keep back" or "preserve". By medieval, the word had taken hold in French as réserver. Kept the meaning of setting something aside. From there it passed into French as réservoir by 1600s-1700s. Applied to a place where water is stored. It reached English by 1600s-1700s as reservoir. Adopted directly from French. Today, reservoir means a natural or artificial lake used to store water for supply.
