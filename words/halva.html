Read the full story

Halva’s story begins in Arabic: halwa meant “sweet.” Literally sweet, a dense confection made from sesame paste or flour and sugar. It reached English by 1800s as halva. Arabic 'sweet'; tahini halva spread through the Ottoman Empire. Today, halva means a dense sweet confection made from sesame paste, flour, or nuts.
