Read the full story

Vicuna’s story begins in Quechua: wik'uña meant “vicuña.” The name for the small, wild Andean camelid prized for its fine wool. By 1500s-1600s, the word had taken hold in Spanish as vicuña. Adopted from Quechua via Spanish colonial contact in the Andes. It reached English by 1700s-1800s as vicuna. Adopted directly from Spanish. Today, vicuna means a wild South American camelid valued for its exceptionally fine wool.
