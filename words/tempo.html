Read the full story

Tempo’s story begins in Latin: tempus meant “time.” Meaning "time". By 1600s, the word had taken hold in Italian as tempo. Applied to the speed of a piece of music. It reached English by 1700s as tempo. Adopted directly from Italian. Today, tempo means the speed or pace at which a piece of music is played.
