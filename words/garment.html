Read the full story

Garment’s story begins in Frankish: warnjan meant “to protect.” A Frankish word related to 'warn'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as garnement. From 'garnir', to equip, the root of 'garnish'. It reached English by 1300s as garment. Used in the Bible's 'hem of his garment'. Today, garment means an item of clothing.
