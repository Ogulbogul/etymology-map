Read the full story

Cheer’s story begins in Late Latin: cara meant “face.” From Greek 'kara', head. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as chiere. 'good cheer' meant a happy face. It reached English by 1200s as cheer. The shout of encouragement dates from the 1700s. Today, cheer means a shout of joy; to make happy.
