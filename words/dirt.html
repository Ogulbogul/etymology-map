Read the full story

Dirt’s story begins in Old Norse: drit meant “excrement, filth.” Excrement, filth. It reached English by 1300s as dirt. Broadened from filth specifically to loose soil or any unclean matter. Today, dirt means loose soil or earth, or any substance that makes something unclean.
