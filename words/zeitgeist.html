Read the full story

Zeitgeist’s story begins in German: Zeit meant “time.” Within German, it grew into Zeitgeist by 1800s. Combining "Zeit" with "Geist" (spirit), coined in German philosophy. It reached English by 1800s as zeitgeist. Adopted directly from German philosophical writing. Today, zeitgeist means the defining spirit or mood of a particular period in history.
