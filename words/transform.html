Read the full story

Transform’s story begins in Latin: forma meant “shape, mold.” Shape, mold, combined with trans- (across). By ancient, the word had taken hold in Latin as transformare. To change shape completely, from trans- and formare. From there it passed into Old French as transformer by medieval. Carried into French for changing something into a different form. It reached English by 1400s as transform. Settled as the English word for changing something completely in form or character. Today, transform means to change something completely in form, appearance, or character.
