Read the full story

Splint’s story begins in Middle Dutch: splinte meant “metal plate, pin.” A thin piece of metal or wood. It reached English by 1300s as splint. First for overlapping plates of armour; the surgical sense followed. Today, splint means a strip of rigid material used to support a broken bone.
