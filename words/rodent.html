Read the full story

Rodent’s story begins in Latin: rodere meant “to gnaw.” The root of 'corrode' and 'erode'. It reached English by 1830s as rodent. Coined by zoologists for the gnawing mammals. Today, rodent means a gnawing mammal such as a rat.
