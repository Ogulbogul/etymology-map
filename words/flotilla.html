Flotilla’s story begins in Spanish: flota meant “fleet.” Within Spanish, it grew into flotilla by medieval. A diminutive meaning "little fleet". It reached English by 1700s as flotilla. Adopted directly from Spanish. Today, flotilla means a small fleet of ships or boats.
