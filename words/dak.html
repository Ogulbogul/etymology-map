Read the full story

Dak’s story begins in Sanskrit: drak meant “quickly.” Meant 'quickly'. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Hindi as dak. Named the relay mail and transport system. It reached English by 1820s as dak. British colonial administrators adopted the word for India's relay postal service. Today, dak means historically, a relay system of mail or passenger transport in South Asia using bearers or horses stationed along a route.
