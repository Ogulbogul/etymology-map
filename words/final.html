Read the full story

Final’s story begins in Late Latin: finalis meant “of the end.” From 'finis', end. By medieval, the word had taken hold in Old French as final. A learned borrowing. It reached English by 1300s as final. Sports 'finals' date from the 1800s. Today, final means coming at the end.
