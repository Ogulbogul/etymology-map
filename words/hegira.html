Read the full story

Hegira’s story begins in Arabic: hajara / hijrah meant “departure, flight.” Meant "to depart"; named Muhammad's flight from Mecca to Medina, the event that starts the Islamic calendar. By 1500s, the word had taken hold in Medieval Latin as hegira. The Islamic calendar counts from the Prophet's migration to Medina in 622. It reached English by 1580s as hegira. Entered English historical and religious writing. Today, hegira means a journey or flight to escape danger, especially with reference to Muhammad's flight from Mecca to Medina in 622 CE.
