        Where does the word "shawarma" come from? It comes from Turkish cevirme, passing through Levantine Arabic before entering English (1900s).

        1. cevirme (Turkish, ottoman): "turning". turning, from cevirmek (to turn), describing meat roasted on a turning spit
        2. shawarma (Levantine Arabic, 1900s): "meat sliced from a rotating spit". adapted into Arabic for meat sliced from a vertical rotating spit
        3. shawarma (English, 1900s): "a dish of spit-roasted meat sliced and served in flatbread". from Turkish 'çevirme', turning, for the rotating spit
